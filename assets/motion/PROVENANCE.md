Three equal-width portraits: confident, impressed, gracious defeat. Brief: an
original silver-haired, dark-skinned bowling custodian in a petrol-blue blazer,
amber glasses and brass details, with an Art Deco club background and intentional
pixel clusters. No third-party reference image, font or branded character used.
Generated service output; no exclusive copyright or legal-clearance claim. This
is a representative presentation study, not completion of the production roster.

`foil.gdshader`: original canvas shader authored for this repository. It uses
pointer position, not device-motion permissions or a time-based flashing field.
Card illustrations and the mural region reuse the E11 sources listed in
docs/art/SOURCES.md and docs/art/ASSET-LEDGER.md. Native font remains the bundled
Godot fallback; no new font package or music/audio asset is introduced.

All screenshots and video are ignored artifacts under artifacts/e12. CI preserves
them as `e12-motion-review`; none are checked into Git as source artwork.
