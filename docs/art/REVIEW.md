# E11 review record

Candidate 01 is awaiting user visual approval. The rendered review fixture is
separate from the live throw lab. No motion, touch usability, phone performance,
or final asset completeness is claimed from these stills.

## Resolved during construction

- Initial generated rack lacked ten distinct heads; regenerated rack has ten
  visible heads in 1/2/3/4 rows with three material families.
- UI lettering is native engine text, not baked/generated wording.
- Five rarity levels have explicit labels and numerical marks as well as color.
- Both aspect ratios preserve the full plate instead of cropping away the ball.

## Open visual/integration defects

1. Generated plate uses finer pixel density than the native frame geometry; final
   cleanup must enforce the bible's common cluster scale. Nearest filtering does
   not make this accepted pixel craft automatically.
2. Ball, pins, lane and reflections are baked together. E12 must separate or
   reconstruct layers before live throws; no score or hitbox can use this plate.
3. Small card metadata needs inspection enlargement at phone scale. The comparison
   board has no interactive hit targets or inspection flow.
4. Landscape uses a comparison grid; the production Charm row, ball bag and
   consumable dock must be demonstrated in E12's integrated layout.
5. Rare border, epic full-card holography and legendary reveal are still references;
   directional response, category frame variants and motion are not yet proven.

These are explicit acceptance gaps. The art direction is delivered for concrete
review; Section 17 and G3 remain open until these defects and E12 are accepted.

## Reviewer checklist

- Inspect `artifacts/e11/portrait.png` (720×1280) and `landscape.png` (1280×720)
  without browser enlargement; also inspect them at half display scale.
- Count all ten heads. Compare the three pin surfaces and five card silhouettes.
- Read target, score, remaining frames and bank; verify nothing covers the ball.
- Evaluate the weakest Common illustration, not only the Legendary treatment.
- Record requested changes or acceptance here; do not infer approval from silence.
