## Pin Counter — `charm.pin_counter`

E19-A13; playable (E18-C01). Rarity: common. Tags: `baseline`, `mult`, `pinfall`.

Authored trigger: +1 Mult for each pin knocked down this frame (all rolls), applied in the Charm phase in slot order. Ten pins give +10 Mult; a frame with no pins down adds nothing.

Required presentation: A score-slip card that ticks up per pin; the log line shows the count (×N pins).

Construction: A narrow ivory bowling score slip, folded at the foot and held by a small brass clip, with a row of broad notched tally marks and tiny pin-shaped stamps. A short dark tally pencil sits diagonally beside it. Tall paper-and-pencil silhouette, no dice, dominoes or framed plus sign.

The score-slip tally increments only for actual distinct pins down across the frame, with the count and +N Mult in the log. Zero stays quiet. Reduced motion uses a static filled tally and the same count. Audio direction for later E20: short dry paper ticks, bounded at ten; no currency sound. Separate the vertical slip/pencil from Flat Bonus's broad framed fixed pips.
