name, exact restriction and one actionable counter in native text beside the art;
the chip opens/identifies the same rule shown before purchases. At 192px card
scale retain material detail; at 48px simplify to the construction specified below.
Current runtime legacy badges use their own bindings; later integration must bind
these declared icons to the requested 48px surface without inventing an in_play ID.

Use the established ivory/brass/petrol/walnut pixel language, deliberate stepped
edges and strong silhouettes. Every rule is Common: consistent rarity border and
text, while identity comes from construction, not a palette change. Preserve the
underlying ball/pin identity under cosmetic rule overlays. No obscured pinfall,
new rival portraits, misleading target rings or full-screen flash.

For subsequent production: generate original transparent illustrations from these
written briefs, preserve source PNGs and exact prompts, and record SHA-256 hashes
in ASSET-LEDGER. Glyph overlays should be original vector/mesh recipes; sounds
original deterministic material foley. No third-party samples are proposed.
Drop final resources under assets/ using the exact IDs below; no assets are
commissioned or marked ready by this document alone.

Reduced motion/flash: stable icon, steady geometry, native rule text and identical
log information; no sheen sweep, pulsing frost, shake or repetitive tint. Sound
obeys mute/volume and bounded priority rules. Effects may annotate authoritative
E18 events only; artwork never calculates aim, physics, eligibility or banked score.

## Oil Slick — `boss_rule.oil_slick`

Tags: `straight`, `precision`, `power`.

Authored trigger: For every roll of the boss match, the ball's hook response is multiplied by 0.5 (the lane's oil and the ball's spin response are unchanged; the curve the same charge produces is halved). Straight throws are unaffected.

Counterplay: Straight-line aim, precision Charms (Plumb Line, House Favorite), or a strong spin ball (Hook Ball) to recover the curve.

Required presentation: A glossy lane sheen readable without colour; the rule badge shows a halved hook arc. The aim preview reflects the reduced curve.

Construction: A low, wide lane strip with three broad stepped specular streaks and a single bent hook arc cut down to half its sweep. Keep the lateral lane edges full-width. Ivory broken highlights on petrol distinguish sheen without hue.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px retain the wide strip, two long glints and shortened curved arrow; omit tiny boards. The preview card shows a full-versus-halved hook arc beside the same full-width lane. Native text says Hook response ×0.5; straight throws unaffected.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: One restrained gloss pass at encounter entry, then a steady half-arc badge. Future aim preview must use the E18-scaled trajectory; the artwork never computes it. Short soft wax-slide sound, no collision or gutter sound. Reduced: static glints and half-arc, identical rule text.

Distinct-type checks: Narrow Lane has two intrusive side rails and a narrow central corridor; Oil Slick has open edges and surface sheen. Dead Straight uses the enclosed plumb/straight-lock badge, while this arc remains visibly curved. Clean Sweep uses a broom/full-rack demand, with no lane sheen.

## Heavy Set — `boss_rule.heavy_set`

Tags: `power`, `spare`, `strike`.

Authored trigger: For the boss match every pin's mass is multiplied by 1.5 (on top of its material's own mass scale). Collisions deflect less, so carry and chain reactions drop.

Counterplay: Hammer Ball's impact, spare-focused scoring (Spare Change, Tip Jar), or Mult builds that need fewer pins.

Required presentation: Pins visibly heavier (iron banding motif) without colour reliance; a heavy thud on impact; the badge shows a weight.

Construction: A squat trapezoid iron weight with a thick handle and a compact upright pin embossed into its face. Broad riveted bands cross the weight below the pin shoulder; short downward load marks frame its base.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px keep the handle, heavy trapezoid and ivory pin cutout; no tiny rivets. Preview card pairs the weight with an iron-banded pin, preserving regulation pin outline. Native text says Pin mass ×1.5 and names the power/spare/Mult counters.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: A brief downward settle of the badge, not movement of the real body; a short low iron/wood thud on authoritative contact after E18. Banding is cosmetic, never hidden collision. Reduced: static loaded base, pin and ×1.5 label.

Distinct-type checks: Unlike Dead Straight's narrow suspended bob, the solid low weight has a broad base. Unlike Clean Sweep's broom/rack motif, it depicts load, not a requirement to clear. Iron banding must not be mistaken for Anchor's standing bonus.

## Frugal House — `boss_rule.frugal_house`

Tags: `baseline`, `mult`, `value`.

Authored trigger: During the boss match, pin, ball and Charm payouts pay nothing (Gold pins, Gold Ball, Tip Jar, Gold Rush's money). Match payout, interest and scoring are unchanged. The suppressed amount is shown in the log.

Counterplay: Scoring strength over economy, and money saved before the boss match.

Required presentation: A closed till motif; suppressed payouts show as struck-through $ amounts.

Construction: A broad closed cash till with a visibly shut drawer, brass key plate and one crossed coin-shaped inset. No roof or clubhouse silhouette. Keep the single drawer seam strong.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px keep the squat till, drawer line and barred coin; no keys or text painted into the icon. Preview card shows the closed till beside an item-payout line struck through. Native text explicitly preserves match payout and interest.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: One drawer-close cue at encounter entry, then a quiet closed state; future suppressed item-income rows show the actual struck-through amount. A muted latch click, never a coin payout jingle. Reduced: fixed closed drawer and unchanged crossed-out native amounts.

Distinct-type checks: House Rules uses the House Ball and a card lock, not currency. Dead Straight's plumb tool and Clean Sweep's broom are unrelated silhouettes. A closed till must not imply that shops, match payout or interest are disabled.

## House Rules — `boss_rule.house_rules`

Tags: `straight`, `baseline`, `house`.

Authored trigger: During the boss match, special-ball cards cannot be armed or played; every roll uses the House Ball. Tactical cards are still playable, and the hand still draws (cards return at the next match).

Counterplay: House Favorite, straight and precision builds, rack materials and Charms instead of special balls.

Required presentation: Special-ball cards in the hand show a clear locked state (shape, not only dimming); the badge shows the House Ball.

Construction: A large House Ball disc with its established groove and finger-hole identity, held in a simple open-bottom card bracket. Two small side card tabs carry lock outlines; the central ball stays unbarred. No roof, till, coins or plumb bob.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px prioritize the round House Ball inside the squared card bracket and one side lock. Preview card shows the House Ball available, special-ball cards locked by shape, and a tactical card visibly available. Native text says House Ball only; tacticals allowed.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: A short card-latch sound when entering the rule. Rejected special-ball selection uses the E18 disabled state, never consumes or visually burns a card. Reduced: stable locks, same accessible House/tactical distinction.

Distinct-type checks: Frugal House is a horizontal cash till; this is a round ball and card bracket. Against Dead Straight, do not add a straight-arrow or claim spin is disabled: the restriction is ball identity. Against Clean Sweep, omit broom/rack-clear symbols. Also separate from One and Done by the latter's single roll pip and terminator bar.

## Charm Freeze — `boss_rule.charm_freeze`

Tags: `mult`, `value`, `order`.

Authored trigger: During the boss match the Charm in slot 1 (leftmost) is silent: its effects do not apply and do not grow. The other Charms apply in order as usual. An empty slot 1 means nothing is lost.

Counterplay: Reorder Charms in the shop before the boss match so the weakest sits in slot 1, or keep slot 1 empty.

Required presentation: Slot 1 visibly frozen (frost overlay, a lock glyph); the shop preview names the rule before purchases so reordering is possible.

Construction: A horizontal three-slot Charm rail with a conspicuous frost-edged lock enclosing only the leftmost slot. Slots two and three stay open with small hanging Charm loops. Deliberate crystalline steps on the first bracket, not a solid blue wash over the whole rail.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px preserve three separated cells and the lock on cell one; use outline/fill differences as well as frost color. Preview card enlarges the leftmost slot and shows the pre-boss reorder counterplay. Native labels identify slot 1 and the unchanged later slots.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: One localized frost closure at boss entry, no repeated screen chill. Suppressed slot stays quiet, including growth; never play its normal activation. Short dry crystalline latch sound. Reduced: fixed lock and stepped frost frame, no spreading frost.

Distinct-type checks: Dead Straight locks curve input with a plumb badge; this locks one explicitly located Charm cell. Clean Sweep demands a full rack clear and uses a broom; this rail is not ten bowling pins. Do not imply all Charms are disabled.

## Narrow Lane — `boss_rule.narrow_lane`

Tags: `precision`, `straight`, `positional`.

Authored trigger: For the boss match each gutter edge moves 2 boards (5.1 cm) toward the centre: a ball crossing the new edge is a gutter ball. Pins, rack and target ring are unchanged.

Counterplay: Precise, straight launches; Sweet Spot and Plumb Line; avoiding wide curves.

Required presentation: Painted guard lines at the new edges visible from the behind-ball view; the aim preview clips at them.

Construction: A tall narrow lane corridor squeezed by two heavy inward-facing guard rails. Two pairs of rectangular board notches sit outside the new edges. Strong parallel edge lines with inward arrows; matte wood center, no shiny surface streaks.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px retain the two inward rails, the open central gap and short inward arrows. Preview card overlays old edges as quiet outlines and new guard lines as heavy marks. Native text says Gutters 2 boards in per side; pins and target unchanged.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: A single inward bracket settle at encounter entry; real gutter lines and clipped aim preview must agree with E18 geometry. Short paired wooden edge knocks, no rolling-slide sound. Reduced: fixed painted guard lines and the same visible corridor, no contraction animation.

Distinct-type checks: Oil Slick has a wide glossy surface and shortened curved arc; this has a narrow matte corridor and explicit side boundaries. Dead Straight's centerline/plumb must not become two rails. Clean Sweep's broad broom head and rack do not belong here.

## One and Done — `boss_rule.one_and_done`

Tags: `strike`, `standing`, `open`.

Authored trigger: In the boss match each frame ends after roll 1: a strike scores as a strike, anything else scores as an open frame with the pins left standing. There are no spares in this match.

Counterplay: Strike builds, or standing-pin builds (Anchor, Last Stand, Lone Survivor) that profit from open frames.

Required presentation: The frame counter shows one roll per frame; the badge shows a single ball.

Construction: One bowling ball crossing a short horizontal frame track toward a blunt end-stop bar. One large ivory roll pip above the track and an empty cutout beyond the stop emphasize that there is no second roll. No House Ball-specific grooves or locked-card stack.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px retain one neutral ball, one pip and the hard stop. Preview card shows a frame strip with a single roll cell; outcome examples are strike or open, with standing pins retained. Native text says One roll per frame; no spares.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: One controlled ball-to-stop tick as the frame explanation ends; do not animate an unearned spare. Short single wood tap, clearly different from a two-roll sound. Reduced: static single cell and stop, with unchanged frame counter.

Distinct-type checks: Dead Straight limits curve, not rolls; avoid its bob/straight-arrow construction. Clean Sweep requires a strike but permits its own roll flow: do not reuse its broom or imply this rule cancels open-frame scoring. House Rules carries recognizable House Ball and card locks; this neutral ball carries only one-roll timing.

## Mult Cap — `boss_rule.mult_cap`

Tags: `value`, `glass`, `backrow`.

Authored trigger: During the boss match a frame's Mult is clamped to at most 20 after the Charm phase, just before banking. Value is not capped. The clamp is shown in the log when it applies.

Counterplay: Value builds: Glass pins, Back Row, Grower, Echo retriggers of Value.

Required presentation: The Mult counter shows a visible ceiling at 20; the clamp step is its own log line.

Construction: A vertical stack of three multiplier bars visibly meeting a thick horizontal ceiling beam. A small multiplication cross sits beneath the beam; the stack stops flush rather than breaking or halving. No pin body or coin.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px keep the ceiling T shape, stepped stack and multiplication cross. Preview card puts a native 20 label on the Mult ceiling and leaves the separate Value column open. Native text says Mult capped at 20 after Charms, before banking.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: One short bounded stop when the actual clamp log row occurs; never cue for an uncapped value below 20. A restrained wooden stop/clack. Reduced: static capped meter and explicit 20 label; same clamp row and original/final Mult.

Distinct-type checks: Dull Pins uses one matte pin and split Value tally, with no ceiling. Dead Straight uses a suspended bob, not a capped meter; Clean Sweep uses a broom/full-rack demand. A ceiling must not read as a target score or a Value cap.

## Dull Pins — `boss_rule.dull_pins`

Tags: `mult`, `charm`, `strike`.

Authored trigger: During the boss match every Value a pin adds (its own and copied or retriggered pin effects) is halved, rounded down per effect. Charm Value and Mult from any source are unchanged.

Counterplay: Mult builds: Charms (Flat Bonus, Finisher, Pin Counter), Ruby pins' Mult.

Required presentation: Pins drawn with a matte, dull finish; halved Value steps marked in the log.

Construction: One upright ivory pin with a broad matte, crosshatched belly and a small two-part Value tally at its foot: one filled block and one outlined block. Keep its recognizable pin profile and avoid cracks, shards or a weight handle.

Preview card / 48px chip: At 48px keep the pin, matte belly band and half-filled two-block tally. Preview card contrasts full and halved pin-Value rows, using native numerals and floor-rounding explanation; Charm Value and all Mult remain visually unbarred.

Activation, sound and reduced direction: At each affected pin-Value row, a short dry brush-down stroke of the tally, no glitter or loss explosion. Soft chalk/wood rub; no glass crack. Reduced: fixed half-tally and matte finish, with the same actual halved Value log.

Distinct-type checks: Mult Cap is a ceiling on a multiplier stack; Dull Pins halves pin Value per effect without capping Mult. Dead Straight's plumb and Clean Sweep's broom must not be reused. Matte finish is an overlay on the underlying material identity, never a recolor that hides Glass/Ruby/Anchor types.
