# E17-D06 — House upgrade art briefs

License category: brass top rail, opposite clipped corners, dark petrol backing;
license tabs on illustrations distinguish these from equipment and tactical cards.
Rarity retains the existing written/symbolic rarity label; uncommon uses a stronger
frame edge. Static identity is the reduced-motion alternative.

## shop_upgrade.punch_card

Ivory punched loyalty card, three used holes, brass punch and bowling seal.

Rule: The first reroll in each Pro Shop visit is free; later rerolls follow the usual $2, $3, ... sequence.

Native treatment: A punched loyalty card on the reroll button; the button shows 'Reroll free' until used.

Slots: icon.shop_upgrade.punch_card, card.shop_upgrade.punch_card, rarity_treatment.shop_upgrade.punch_card, activation.shop_upgrade.punch_card, sound.shop_upgrade.punch_card, reduced_motion.shop_upgrade.punch_card.

## shop_upgrade.pin_caddy

Teal leather caddy, three padded pockets, two wood pins and a clear empty third pocket.

Rule: Adds a third reserve slot for pins for the rest of the attempt. Reserves hold pins between matches and feed Reserve Call.

Native treatment: A caddy case beside the rack summary with three pockets; the new pocket is visibly added on purchase.

Slots: icon.shop_upgrade.pin_caddy, card.shop_upgrade.pin_caddy, rarity_treatment.shop_upgrade.pin_caddy, activation.shop_upgrade.pin_caddy, sound.shop_upgrade.pin_caddy, reduced_motion.shop_upgrade.pin_caddy.

## shop_upgrade.clearance_tag

Red clipped price tag, brass eyelet, downward arrow and coin; colour is not the sole cue.

Rule: Every Pro Shop offer, including the License offer, costs $1 less, never below $1. Sale values stay half the price actually paid, so nothing can be resold at a profit.

Native treatment: A red clearance tag on every offer price; the struck-through original price shows beside the new one.

Slots: icon.shop_upgrade.clearance_tag, card.shop_upgrade.clearance_tag, rarity_treatment.shop_upgrade.clearance_tag, activation.shop_upgrade.clearance_tag, sound.shop_upgrade.clearance_tag, reduced_motion.shop_upgrade.clearance_tag.

## shop_upgrade.savings_book

Ivory ledger pages in a green passbook, five brass tally tabs for the raised cap.

Rule: Raises the interest cap from $3 to $5: interest still pays $1 per $5 held when a match ends, now up to $5.

Native treatment: A passbook in the settlement line; the interest row shows the raised cap.

Slots: icon.shop_upgrade.savings_book, card.shop_upgrade.savings_book, rarity_treatment.shop_upgrade.savings_book, activation.shop_upgrade.savings_book, sound.shop_upgrade.savings_book, reduced_motion.shop_upgrade.savings_book.

## shop_upgrade.extra_shelf

Walnut shelf extension with brass brackets; distinct sixth bay silhouette.

Rule: The Pro Shop shows one more offer every visit, drawn from any category it sells (Charm, ball, pin, tactical card or modification), from the next stock onward.

Native treatment: A sixth shelf with its own 'Extra Shelf' label among the offers.

Slots: icon.shop_upgrade.extra_shelf, card.shop_upgrade.extra_shelf, rarity_treatment.shop_upgrade.extra_shelf, activation.shop_upgrade.extra_shelf, sound.shop_upgrade.extra_shelf, reduced_motion.shop_upgrade.extra_shelf.

## shop_upgrade.spotlight_sign

Brass theatre spotlight on a dark shop sign, focused cream cone and rarity stars.

Rule: Uncommon and rare offers come up more often: rarity odds become common 40%, uncommon 38%, rare 18%, epic 4% (from 55/30/12/3), from the next stock onward.

Native treatment: A spotlight over the offer grid; rarity frames glow brighter while it is owned.

Slots: icon.shop_upgrade.spotlight_sign, card.shop_upgrade.spotlight_sign, rarity_treatment.shop_upgrade.spotlight_sign, activation.shop_upgrade.spotlight_sign, sound.shop_upgrade.spotlight_sign, reduced_motion.shop_upgrade.spotlight_sign.
