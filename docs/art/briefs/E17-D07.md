# E17-D07 — House Charm briefs

AV-H1-A covers only Head Pin, Wood Grain and Opening Act. Each needs icon,
card, rarity_treatment, reduced_motion, activation and sound slots under its
exact charm ID. Common uses the existing square silver Charm frame; uncommon
uses the existing softly rounded brass frame. Words and symbols retain rarity.

## charm.head_pin

Crown-less ivory pin at the apex of a triangular petrol rack badge. Nine small
positions below it represent the rest of the rack. Native treatment must light
position 1 when its resolver event counts, regardless of material or roll.
Reduced motion uses a static highlight. Proposed cue: short wood knock.

## charm.wood_grain

Honey walnut plank medallion, carved grain and an inset plain Wood Pin.
Native treatment must add one grain line per counted Wood Pin, using resolver
facts/events; never count animation completions. Static lines and written count
remain in reduced motion. Proposed cue: dry wooden scrape. Preserve Old Lou's
future first-clear binding; art does not change unlock configuration.

## charm.opening_act

Burgundy curtain-up proscenium, brass marquee bulbs and an ivory pin on stage.
Native treatment must light the marquee in frame 1 and dim it in frame 2,
reading authoritative frame context. Reduced motion uses the same two static
states. Proposed cue: short curtain swish with a brass accent.

AV-H1-A delivers all six slots and the native counted/conditional displays.
Native evidence and final verification are recorded in handoffs/AV-H1-A.md.
AV-H1-B's other three briefs belong to its later session.

## charm.open_house — AV-H1-B

Open walnut door in petrol/brass surround with burgundy welcome mat. Common
Charm frame. Resolver's open-frame event opens the hinged door; reduced motion
holds a static open state. Original short wooden hinge cue. Slots: icon, card,
rarity_treatment, reduced_motion, activation, sound under charm.open_house.

## charm.strike_fund — AV-H1-B

Petrol glass savings jar, slotted brass lid marked X and brass coin. Common
Charm frame. Only its after-bank strike payout drops a single coin; income stays
in the authoritative log. Static coin pose in reduced motion. Original metallic
coin cue. Same six slots under charm.strike_fund.

## charm.holdouts — AV-H1-B

Stubborn ivory pins with folded wooden arms and red neck bands. Common Charm
frame. Activation draws one folded-arm figure per resolver standing count,
bounded by ten rack positions, with no animation-derived count. Static figures
in reduced motion. Original low wooden cue. Same six slots under charm.holdouts.
