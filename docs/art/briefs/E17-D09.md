## Spare Ball

Plain petrol plastic with one straight ivory stripe and an amber seam. Grouped
finger holes, satin resin, clean silhouette; no marbling or implied spin trail.
In play, the stripe is translation-relative and world-oriented: physical body
rotation never rotates the stripe. Existing 0.5 spin response is unchanged.
Eligible spare resolver event only: steady parallel guide lines frame the
identity, +4 Mult remains in the native score explanation. Original short
rubber/plastic knock (660 Hz, 0.24 s). Static identity and lines in reduced mode.

## Workhorse

Oxblood league resin with deliberate scratches, rubbed satin patches and a worn
cream shield. No tally marks baked into the illustration. In play, object-local
scuffs rotate with the real ball; existing mass scale 1.15 is unchanged.
One scored tally per resolver event.count, in two groups of five. Count and roll
identity come from the committed resolver event/source, never elapsed time,
contact count or animation state. +2 Value per pin, per Workhorse roll is the
rule; the score log retains its explicit numeric calculation. Original low
wood/resin knock (240 Hz, 0.32 s), shared bounded mixer. Static complete tally
in reduced mode; mute and skipped-step semantics stay in the existing director.

## Oak Pin

Dense dark honey oak with tight grain, two walnut neck rings, an acorn carving
in the illustration and visible endgrain. Distinct from pale enamel Wood Pin.
The existing 1.3 mass is authoritative. Native visual mesh adds a small damped
rock (maximum 0.035 rad, two-second envelope) after an actual rigid-body contact;
never write collision geometry, velocity, body transform or score. Reduced
motion/flash suppresses the cosmetic rock. A damped low arc surrounds the
identity on its +20 Value fallen event, held still in reduced mode. Original
low hardwood knock (170 Hz, 0.46 s).
