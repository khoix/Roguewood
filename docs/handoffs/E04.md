## Changes and decisions
- **Physics** (`src/simulation/`): Jolt at 120 Hz on a regulation lane with a seamless surface, arc gutters, a cap, set-back kickbacks and a pit. There are ten Wood pins (regulation profile as two hulls, custom center of mass) and the House ball. Each roll runs in a fresh World3D, and the physical ball enters at the launch tick. Pins are credited once by tilt over 45° or leaving the deck, never resurrected; leaning past 12° at the end also counts as down. The roll ends on settle or timeout. Attribution uses the first contact that carried impulse (ball, pin chain or environment). A ball that leaves the lane before touching a pin is a gutter ball: its pinfall is voided and respotted. Survivors are respotted upright for roll 2.
- **Input** (`src/input/`): a pure hold/drag/flick recognizer. Only the final 100 ms of motion steers the throw. Short, sideways, slow, OS-cancelled or interrupted gestures cancel safely. The mapper builds a contract `launch` with spin 0. Thresholds are provisional.
- **Presentation** (`src/presentation/`): a throw lab with an elevated behind-ball aim camera, a follow camera that moves to a deck view, status text, Next Rack, and developmental lit geometry (lathe pins, finger-holed ball, foul line, arrows). It sits behind the shell's session bar; Restart Session and Clubhouse still drive J01.
- **Contracts**: a new versioned `knockdown_log` with semantic validation and `validate_log_for_result`. Enum checks now compare numbers by value, which fixes an int record wrongly failing against a JSON `1.0`. Rack state stays simulation-internal until E10.
- Settled decisions are in [ADR 002](../decisions/002-throw-lab-physics.md): the measured Jolt combine rules (friction min, bounce sum), the raised angular-velocity limit, the geometry fixes found by testing (seam hops, gutter escape, speculative ghost hits), the gutter rule, the fall and settle thresholds, and the input mapping. Mouse→touch emulation is enabled.
- Content completeness flags: unchanged. There are no production items, and the Wood pin and House ball are lab fixtures, not catalogue entries.
