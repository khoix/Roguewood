## What changed
- **Lab** (src/presentation/throw_lab.gd):
  - **Model swap:** the held-ball model replaces the E05 gesture, which is deleted (`src/input/flick_gesture.gd`).
  - **Timing:** gesture samples carry event time in real play. When a journey drives the game (`--script` with `--fixed-fps`), they use the frame clock, so automated gestures stay deterministic.
  - **Every frame:** the lab calls `tick()` and draws the held ball. The ball follows the spring sideways along the approach, lifts as the finger rises, spins at the live charge and leans into its motion. Reduced motion keeps it steady.
  - **Cue signal:** `throw_cue(id, charge)` fires `throw.charge`, `throw.curve_ready` and `throw.full_charge` once each per hold, for AV-T01.
  - **Vibration:** 25 ms at curve-ready and 45 ms at full charge, unless the new Vibration setting is off.
  - **Meter readers:** `held_charge()` (raw), `curve_ready()` and `full_charge()` feed the meter. `charged_spin()` is now what will be thrown, which is zero below the threshold.
- **Overlay** (aim_overlay.gd):
  - The meter shows the charge building. It stays dim below the threshold and reads "Charging".
  - At the threshold it lights up, reads "Curve ↻ n%" and shows a placeholder sparkle of orbiting diamonds. At full charge it turns gold and reads "Full".
  - AV-T01 replaces the placeholder sparkle with final effects.
- **Settings** (throw_settings_panel.gd):
  - A "Vibration on charge" toggle.
  - A "Throw tuning (test)…" page with sliders for charge per radian, full-rate swing speed, spin half-life, ball spring and spring damping, plus "Reset to defaults" and "Back".
  - Values live in `settings.throw_tuning` for the session (persisting settings is E25 work) and reach the live model at once.
- **Trial log:** gesture throws record `model: "held"`, the raw `charge` and the `tuning` in force.
- **Model** (held_ball.gd): flicks within 2° of straight up throw exactly straight (`straight_zone_deg`), so leftover spring motion never nudges an intended straight ball. A unit test covers it.
- **Codex paths:** motion_director.gd and ability_director.gd read the pin tracker before the launch's first physics tick, when the lab already reads "rolling". Both now wait for the tracker. Behaviour is unchanged once it exists.
- **Tried and dropped:** disabling input accumulation, to get every finger sample rather than one merged drag per frame, broke keyboard focus on the assist sliders (J03, J06). The model works on merged samples, so accumulation stays on.

## Journeys
- **J02** (tests/throw_journey.gd) now checks:

  - The held ball travels with the circling finger, and the meter is curve-ready mid-hold.
  - The `throw.charge` and `throw.curve_ready` cues fire in order.
  - A weak swing charges but throws exactly straight, with no curve-ready cue.
