## What changed
The model is described in ADR 011, "Revision: the flywheel".
- **The flywheel:** the held ball's spin is its own state with momentum, kicked by the area the finger's path sweeps.
  - It winds down at a half-life and never passes a maximum.
  - The charge is the spin against that maximum, and the ball is drawn turning at exactly that spin.
- **Spin model** on Options → Throw tuning (test):
  - 1, swing + flywheel (A): the default.
  - 2, spin in place (B).
  - 0, the earlier orbit charge (E05-R4), kept for comparison.
- **New tuning sliders:**
  - spin from circling (gain);
  - spin half-life;
  - spin at full charge;
  - path window;
  - a vibration tick per turn (off by default).
- **Unchanged:** the flick, the release window, the curve threshold, the launch, the physics and the scoring.
