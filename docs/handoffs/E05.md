## Changes and decisions (see [ADR 003](../decisions/003-spin-curve-input.md))
- **Spin phase:** circling charges signed spin from how far the path's direction turns. A 200° dead zone, two loops for full charge, and ignoring reversals and jitter mean placement drags never spin. Turning inside the final 100 ms release window is excluded, so the circle's tangent can't steer or spin the flick. The ball stops following the finger once circling starts. Sensitivity runs from ×0.5 to ×2.0.
- **Sign:** positive spin is clockwise as the bowler sees it and hooks right. A shot is curved when |spin| ≥ 0.25.
- **Hook physics:** a true forward roll plus side rotation (15 rad/s at full spin) through a 40 ft oil pattern applied as the ball's friction: 0.002 in the oil, 0.2 in the backend, 0.12 on the deck. The ball skids straight, then breaks. Measured hook: 9–28 cm fast, 13–40 cm medium, 23–66 cm slow. Results mirror exactly, and faster throws hook less. E04's straight outcomes and ticks are unchanged.
- **Target ring:** tap or drag the lane to move it. It contracts 0.30→0.06 over 1.6 s while held, freezes at release and travels with the launch. The grade colour is shown. It is drawn as a magnified reticle because its true size is about 7 px at 20 m. A short qualitative guide and a live spin meter show while aiming; the aim ball spins unless reduced motion is on.
- **Accessibility:** handedness, sensitivity, reduced motion (steady clean-only ring) and larger touch targets. Assist controls (Position, Aim, Power, Spin, Target, Throw) work through keyboard or gamepad focus and emit records identical to gestures. Settings survive Restart Session; saving them is E25 work.
- **Devices:** mouse input through touch emulation, tagged in the logs. Stretch aspect `expand` so landscape fills the screen. A JSONL trial log (`user://throw_trials.jsonl`). A written device-trial protocol.
- Contracts: no schema change. The launch semantics for spin sign, curve threshold, target and ring are now documented in CONTRACTS.md.
