## Changes and decisions
- **Frame assembly:** `FrameBuilder` builds a `FrameRules` frame from the completed frame's roll reports (strike, or two rolls). It revalidates every report contract and hydrates all effects by stable ID from a catalogue. The lab uses `content/fixtures/lab_catalogue.json` (Wood pin, House ball): a fixture outside the production registry, with all completeness flags false.
- **Resolution:** the unchanged E02 `FrameRules`. The fixed order, a single best-accuracy award, bounded retriggers (≤ 8) and refusing to overflow were already pure. E06 adds tests at the retrigger and overflow boundaries.
- **Readable resolution:** `ScoreLog` turns the authoritative events into ordered steps with text, symbol (+ / × / = / →), the pin to highlight, and the resolver's running Value and Mult.
- **Score panel:** reveals the steps one at a time at 1×/2×/4× (the choice is kept in session settings) or Skip. It shows Value × Mult counters, a flat ten-slot rack coloured by the roll that took each pin (outline = stood) with the active pin ringed, and a persistent auto-scrolling log.
- **Flow:** after the frame's last roll the lab enters `scoring` and then `frame_done` (banked total, Next Rack). The shell's session fixture tracks the score and frame. Presentation copies steps and cannot rescore.
- Visual fixes made after inspecting the rendered captures: the assist panel hides outside aiming (it overlapped the panel); the log scrolls after layout (the Bank line was hidden); the panel background is opaque; pin numbers sit inside the circles, with a legend.
