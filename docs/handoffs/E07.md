## Changes and decisions
- **Models:** `CardDeck` implements §15 exactly:
  - A four-card hand; keep one and cycle one per frame.
  - Ball cards go to the discard pile; tactical cards are exhausted, one per roll.
  - A seeded reshuffle on the deck's own stream, and a recombine at match start that excludes exhausted cards.
  - House sits outside the deck.
  - JSON round trip including RNG state.

  `DirectLoadout` is the original comparison model (House plus two specials with two uses per match). Both share one interface.
- **Lab:** a card row (`card_hand.gd`).
  - Tap, click or keyboard to arm a ball and a tactic. Cycle, then a card. ★ KEEP after a frame (not at match end).
  - Cards lock while rolling; cancelled or rejected throws spend nothing. Cards are spent only after `LaneWorld.commit` succeeds.
  - Options → Equipment switches models. The switch takes effect now on a fresh frame, otherwise at Next Rack, and starts a new match.
  - The lab plays two-frame matches (§2), seeded from the session seed (1).
  - The trial log records the equipment model, the card and tactic played, the hand, cycle and keep, and pile counts for each roll.
- **Scoring:** played tactics join the roll's effects through `FrameBuilder.build(..., tactics)`, and `owners` credits each log step to its card.
- **Rendered fixes:**
  - The direct-mode use count is moved onto its own card line (it was clipped).
  - In landscape the row is a two-row panel away from the assist side, so it does not cover the ball (found by J02's landscape throw failing against the first layout).
- **Defect found by J04 and fixed:** after a Lab Coin payout step (after bank), the score panel dropped the banked "= score" line. Payout effects were also credited to the wrong card when combined with a tactic, because deferred events were counted in the same sequence as immediate ones. `ScoreLog` now keeps the bank on after-bank steps and counts deferred and immediate effects separately. A unit case covers it; a mutation check confirmed the case fails without the fix.
- **Decision:** provisional, in ADR 004. The draw-and-cycle deck stays the default and direct selection stays a lab option. The E13 human vertical-slice playtest decides, using the criteria in the ADR.
