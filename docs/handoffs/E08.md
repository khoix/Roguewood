## Changes and decisions (details in ADR 005)
- **Economy rules** (`economy.gd`, all provisional):
  - The run starts with $6.
  - Match payout is $3 plus $1 per unused frame, with interest of $1 per $5 held (capped at $3) computed before the payout.
  - Rerolls cost $2, rising by $1 within a visit; removal costs $3.
  - Sales return half the price actually paid; free items sell for the catalogue's base value.
- **Run inventory** (`run_inventory.gd`):
  - The wallet and ledger are run-scoped, with per-match and per-run stats.
  - The rack has ten positions plus two reserves; Charms have five ordered slots.
  - The deck holds up to 16 cards, with at most two copies per identity.
  - Modifications: one per item, compatibility checked against the host.
  - Placements are explicit, and a replacement sells what it replaces. Rack pins are not sellable.
  - Item payouts are credited from FrameRules once per activation. Used tactical cards leave the run at match end.
- **Shop** (`shop.gd`):
  - A dedicated seeded stream generates five slots.
  - Eligibility is schema-driven: pool weight and legendary, unlock, Division, copy limit, incompatibility, modification host, and no duplicates.
  - Rarity uses 55/30/12/3/0 renormalized over eligible rarities, then pool weight.
  - Rerolls avoid the previous offers wherever the pool allows. An affordable offer is guaranteed when one exists.
  - `plan` previews a purchase; refusals change nothing. Every pick is in the selection log, and the shop round-trips through JSON.
- **Lab:** Next Rack after a match's second frame settles it and opens the Pro Shop (`shop_panel.gd`):
  - The settlement explanation and the five offers (slot, rarity, name, effect, price, and SOLD or a reason).
  - Reroll, removal, Sell buttons, and a choice sheet for placements, hosts and replacements, with Cancel.
  - Next Match continues. The lab's rack, Charms, deck and modifications come from the inventory, so purchases score.
  - Money is shown in the status line and tracked by the session.
- **Fixtures:** Lab Gold and Lab Lead (locked) pins; Lab Tally, Purse and Miser Charms (Purse and Miser incompatible), Lab Double (Division 2) and Lab Crown (legendary); the Lab Spark tactical card; Lab Gilded and Oak pin modifications and the Lab Wax ball modification. Lab balls now cost $5. All completeness flags are false.
- **Defects found while building and fixed:**
  - Removing a button inside its own touch or mouse release raised an engine `can_process` error. Sell, choice and card buttons now emit deferred.
  - Unit tests first missed a broken reroll rule and a broken affordability guarantee (mutation checks). Both tests were rewritten until the mutations failed them.
