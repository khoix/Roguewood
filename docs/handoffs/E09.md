## Changes and decisions (details in ADR 006)
- **DivisionRun:**
  - Opener 300, feature 500 and boss 800, each within two frames.
  - A match is won the moment its banked total reaches the target (after that frame resolves) and lost when the budget runs out.
  - Losing ends the attempt; the boss win ends the Division.
  - Practice mode has no targets. The spec is validated and the state serializes.
- **The Custodian (Clean Sweep):** an open frame banks 0. It is applied to the match's banked score, not the FrameRules result, with an explanatory ✕ log step. Unknown rules are refused.
- **Previews:** the boss badge in the match bar from the first frame (inspectable: rule, target, first-clear reward), and a "next match / boss" preview in every shop.
- **Rival presentation:**
  - A skippable introduction: 6 s first time, 2 s afterwards, no fade under reduced motion.
  - Reactions with precedence and cooldown. The bubble ignores input and sits clear of the ball and rack.
  - The portrait is a developmental badge.
- **Results:**
  - The per-match table.
  - Loss: the unmet score and the rule in force.
  - Win: the first-clear reward banked at once into the in-memory Career record (`gate.lab_rival`, which unlocks Lab Lead in the shop).
  - Retry or New Attempt (next seed), and Clubhouse. Restart Session stays attempt 1 (J01 determinism).
- **Practice option:** J02–J05 use it so they keep testing their own systems.
- **Defects found while building and fixed:**
  - The match bar covered the first status line and the shop header (both moved down).
  - The shop preview doubled its punctuation.
  - J06's wait lambdas captured the freed lab after Retry and raised engine errors; they now compare instance ids.
