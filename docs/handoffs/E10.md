## Changes and decisions (details in ADR 007)
- **Save store:**
  - A checksummed envelope with canonical JSON (sorted, full precision).
  - Atomic write: temp file, verify, back up, replace.
  - Fallback to the backup; quarantine of unreadable files; player-readable notices.
  - Schema versions with a one-step migration registry; refuses newer, zero or gapped versions; quiet parsing.
- **Attempt snapshot:** one transaction per committed step (aim, committed, roll_resolved, frame_done, shop, intro, division_over, plus backgrounding).
  - It carries RNG and deck piles, the shop (with its own unlocked pool), inventory and ledger, the Division, exact rack positions, this frame's reports, played cards and modifications, and the pending launch.
  - Presentation and settings are not saved; on resume the saved equipment model and practice mode win.
- **Recovery:**
  - A committed launch re-simulates unchanged with its cards already spent.
  - A banked frame is shown again but never re-banked.
  - Shop purchases and rerolls are neither repeated nor refunded.
  - Roll two resumes on the exact standing pins.
- **Career file:** separate from the attempt. Awards are idempotent and written before the attempt records the victory.
- **Shell:** Continue Attempt (focused when available), Enter League starts fresh, and notices explain fallbacks and unusable saves.
- **Defects found while building and fixed:**
  - A frame's total was added only after its animation, so a save during the animation would have lost it. It is now banked before the frame_done checkpoint.
  - New attempts shared the Career's `unlocked` array, so a later unlock would have changed a running attempt's pool; the attempt now takes a copy.
  - The shop's selection log came back from JSON with float fields.
  - J07 itself had to allow a one-strike boss win, because a Charm bought earlier in the journey (correctly) scores.
