## Changes and decisions (details in ADR 010)
- **Unlock registry:** stable IDs for every gate (boss, championship, mastery), the Leagues (content set, access, championship award), three provisional mastery objectives and counter tags per boss rule. Boss gates come from rivals' `first_clear.unlocks`.
- **Graph validation**, run by `qa.py content` and loaded by the shell:
  - unique, prefixed IDs; declared gates of one kind, each awarded by a source of that kind; item unlock kinds matching their gates;
  - no loops; everything reachable from a fresh Career;
  - no objective needing the pins it unlocks;
  - initial counters to every boss rule in each open League;
  - an availability report (production: balls 4/0, pins 6/0, Charms 10 initial and 2 locked).
- **Career schema 2:** awards with provenance, attempt uids, championships, mastery, totals, the attempt tally, per-League records (practice apart) and item history. Collection states are Locked, Available, Discovered and Mastered. v1 migrates; a golden v2 file is frozen.
- **Banking:** each award is saved the moment it is earned, before the attempt checkpoint, and idempotently: a gate once, a frame once per uid, a title once per uid. A later title is a record, not a repeat unlock.
- **Pool snapshots:** an attempt keeps the gates it began with (saved with its shop); unlocks join the pool from the next attempt, and the result says so.
- **Practice policy:** discoveries and practice records only; no first clears, titles, mastery or unlocks. The Practice toggle and the match bar state it before the first throw.
- **Shell:** the menu shows the Career summary and up to three goals, disables a League whose access gates are not held (both current Leagues are open), and reports gates the current version no longer declares, which are kept.
- **No vertical progression:** the Career never touches score, payout, interest or starting money.
