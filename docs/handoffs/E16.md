## Changes and decisions
- **Clubhouse** (`src/clubhouse/clubhouse.gd`) is the home screen, with four tabs. It reads the Career only through `clubhouse_model.gd`, which is pure and unit-tested.
  - **League Board:** the shell's League, kit, Continue and Enter controls, the Career summary and followed goals, each League's record (attempts, best Division, best total, best frame, titles, practice best), any access lock, and the three latest unlocks.
  - **Binder:** every collectible (balls, pins, Charms, tactical, mods, upgrades, kits; boss rules appear as rival plaques). Each shows Locked, Available, Discovered or Mastered, with category filters. Inspecting a card shows what unlocks it (named rival or League, or goal), its history (attempts, League wins), its tradeoff, and the reminder that a new attempt never starts with it.
  - **Trophies:** League titles, mastery ribbons, a plaque per rival (none for the campaign's provisional encounters) and club decorations. Each is earned or "not yet", with what earns it.
  - **Goals:** every mastery objective with progress (Career totals; single-attempt goals show 0 between attempts). The player can follow up to three goals, saved in `career.tracked`; the board and results show the followed goals first.
- **Navigation:**
  - Returning to the Clubhouse always opens the League Board, with Continue (resume) or Enter focused.
  - Enter reads "Try Again" once the League has been played (design §16 button words).
  - Results keep Retry and Clubhouse.
- **Club decorations** (design §16, no gameplay effect) are in the unlock registry as `cosmetics`: `deco.club_sign` (1 first clear), `deco.lane_trim` (1 mastery goal), `deco.trophy_case` (1 title). The graph validates their IDs and milestones.
- **No new currency** and no grind: everything shown is derived from E15 records.
- **Readability:** the Clubhouse uses 22 px text on the 720-wide portrait canvas; headings are 30 px, and buttons are at least 52–56 px tall. Physical-phone readability remains a device gate (E26).
- **Art by asset ID** (`clubhouse_art.gd`):
  - A slot draws `res://assets/clubhouse/<asset id>.png` if present, or the catalogue's `assets.card` value when that is a `res://` path.
  - Otherwise it draws a marked placeholder naming the asset ID.
  - Dropping files in is enough; no code change is needed.
