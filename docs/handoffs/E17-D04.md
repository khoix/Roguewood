## Decisions
- **How a modification works** (already built in E08): a ball modification attaches to one ball card and its effects join each roll that card is thrown; a pin modification attaches to one rack position and its effects join that pin's. One modification per host. The batch only adds definitions.
- **Roles:** every family has an answer here: spare (Chalk Grip), curve (Spin Wax), economy (Coin Slot, Gilt Leaf), retrigger (Echo Charge), power and straight lines (Lead Core), consistency (Bumper Guard), Mult (Brass Cap), standing builds (Lantern), Value and strikes (Keystone, Lead Weight) and precision (Spearhead, the first-fallen mirror of Crown Pin).
- **Contradictions** are refused by `incompatibilities`: Echo Charge on Echo Ball, Lead Core on Hammer Ball, Gilt Leaf on Gold Pin, Lead Weight on Anchor Pin.
- **Unlock rewards:** True Curve and Mixed Rack now also gate Spin Wax and Keystone. Their reward text names them when E18 lands each one (as True Curve's names Banana Ball).
- **Primitives:** nine use existing pieces. Three primitives remain: physics.mod_mass_scale (Lead Core, Lead Weight, one session), physics.gutter_rebound (Bumper Guard) and condition.first_fallen (Spearhead, a small extension of C03's last_fallen).
