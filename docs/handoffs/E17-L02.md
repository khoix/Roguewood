## The Neon League (content/leagues/neon.json)
- **Identity** (design §16): curve and precision, on lanes that visibly change, with straight lines kept viable. Open from the start, like the House League.
- **Eight Divisions** of opener, feature and boss, in the House spec's format. Targets follow the House League's curve (provisional until E24).
- **Bosses:** Slick (Oil Slick), Laser Lena (Narrow Lane), DJ Flicker (Strobe), in that order, three times; Division 8 is the Championship.
- **Champion:** DJ Flicker, Neon Champion: Strobe plus Oil Slick, on Two-Tone. The halved hook and the halved ring zones press both curve and accuracy; Two-Tone's dry left half gives back some curve, and a straight, powerful game still scores.
- **Lanes:** the pool is House Oil, Long Oil and Two-Tone. Openers keep House Oil. Features play a Neon lane from Division 2 (a new allowance: the validator checks a feature's lanes come from the pool too).

## E17-D11: the definitions the spec needs (3)
- **Long Oil** (`lane_condition.long_oil`, primitive `lane.oil_length`): the oil runs to 47 ft instead of 40, so a charged spin curves later and less. Straight throws are unaffected.
- **Two-Tone** (`lane_condition.two_tone`, primitive `lane.oil_sides`): on the left half of the lane the oil ends at 34 ft, so a ball rolling there hooks earlier and further; the right half is standard. This replaces a "short oil" idea, which would only have changed the same number as Long Oil (the batch check refuses that as a recolor) and repeated Dry Backend's "more hook". Two-Tone is the visible lane variation the design asks for, and it makes the line a choice.
- **Neon Kit** (`kit.neon`, `kit.loadout`).

The three Neon rules were written earlier: Oil Slick and Narrow Lane in E17-D03, Strobe in E17-D05.
