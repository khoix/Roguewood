## E17-D11: the definitions the spec needs (3)
- **Long Oil** (`lane_condition.long_oil`, primitive `lane.oil_length`): the oil runs to 47 ft instead of 40, so a charged spin curves later and less. Straight throws are unaffected.
- **Two-Tone** (`lane_condition.two_tone`, primitive `lane.oil_sides`): on the left half of the lane the oil ends at 34 ft, so a ball rolling there hooks earlier and further; the right half is standard. This replaces a "short oil" idea, which would only have changed the same number as Long Oil (the batch check refuses that as a recolor) and repeated Dry Backend's "more hook". Two-Tone is the visible lane variation the design asks for, and it makes the line a choice.

## E18-C13: the rules and lanes
`RivalRules.lane_rules(rules)` says what the rules in force change in the roll; the lab sets it on each new lane, again at commit (so a Rule Waiver played while aiming counts) and on resume (so a restart re-simulates the same roll). With no Neon rule it is empty, and the lane is exactly the standard one.
- **Oil Slick** (`hook_scale` 0.5): the side spin a charge produces is halved in the lane; the committed spin is kept, so a curved throw still counts as curved for Charms and the log. The aim guide bends half as far. Measured on `curve_right_50`: 0.132 m of hook against 0.227 m.
- **Narrow Lane** (`gutter_edge`, two of the lane's 39 boards in: 0.473 m from the centre instead of 0.527).
  - It is a ruling, not new geometry: moving the physical gutters would leave the corner pins' bases over them. A ball that crosses the edge before touching a pin is a gutter ball, as the rule says.
  - A rail then carries it sideways into the real gutter (1.2 m/s, once), so it never rolls on into pins that cannot count.
  - Launches are kept inside the edges (start up to 0.693 instead of 0.795), and magenta guard lines show the edges while aiming (developmental; the rule art replaces them).
  - The design text said 5.1 cm; two boards are 5.4 cm, and the text is corrected.
- **Strobe** (`zone_scale` 0.5): halving the perfect and sweet radii outright would put perfect (0.04) below the ring's smallest radius (0.06), so it could never be earned. Instead each band is halved from that floor: perfect to 0.07 (was 0.08), sweet to 0.11 (was 0.16). Both grades stay reachable with a later, tighter release, and clean and miss grading are unchanged. The ring's colour and the roll's grade both use it. The design text now says so exactly.
- **Long Oil:** the oil ends at 14.33 m (47 ft). Measured: 0.168 m of hook against 0.227 m.
- **Two-Tone:** on the left of the centre board the oil ends at 10.36 m (34 ft). A ball that stays on the right rolls exactly as on House Oil; a left curve hooks 0.280 m against 0.227 m.
- The status line names each Neon rule in force and the match's lane. Lane names now come from the catalogue in League content (the shop preview showed raw ids).
