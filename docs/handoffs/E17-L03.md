## The Salvage League (content/leagues/salvage.json)
- **Identity** (design §16: "Material interactions, modifications, and repair/replacement decisions"): the pressure is the build itself. Each boss takes one support away, so the player decides what to keep, repair or replace:
  - **Miser Moll, Frugal House:** items pay no money in her match (match pay and interest still come).
  - **Grimewell, Dull Pins:** every pin's Value is halved, its own and any it copies.
  - **Scrapjack, Rust:** every modification on balls and pins is switched off; the base items still work.
  - The counterplay is Charms, Mult and strong base equipment, all available from the start.
- **Open from the start**, like House and Neon. The title (`gate.salvage_champion`) opens the Foundry League.
- **Eight Divisions** of opener, feature and boss, in the House format. Targets follow the House League's curve (provisional until E24).
- **Lanes:** the pool is House Oil, Grit and Warped Boards. Openers keep House Oil; features play a Salvage lane from Division 2, and bosses from Division 3.

| Division | Feature lane | Boss | Boss lane |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | House Oil | Miser Moll | House Oil |
| 2 | Grit | Grimewell | House Oil |
| 3 | Warped Boards | Scrapjack | Warped Boards (no mods to correct the drift) |
| 4 | Grit | Miser Moll | Grit |
| 5 | Warped Boards | Grimewell | Warped Boards |
| 6 | Grit | Scrapjack | Grit (a light base ball loses the most) |
| 7 | Warped Boards | Miser Moll | Warped Boards |
| 8 | Grit | Scrapjack, Salvage Champion | Grit |

- **Champion:** Scrapjack, Salvage Champion: Rust plus Dull Pins, on Grit. Modifications are off and pin Value is halved, so Charms and their Mult carry the score, and a heavy base ball carries through the grit.
- **Rivals:** full identities for all four (an intro naming the rule and its counterplay, six reactions, a first clear). Their gates are `gate.miser_moll_cleared`, `gate.grimewell_cleared`, `gate.scrapjack_cleared` and `gate.salvage_champion_cleared`; as with Neon, they are registered when the League goes live, and the first-clear items are chosen in the item batches.
- **Kit:** the Salvage Kit (initial), $8. A scrap rack of six Wood, two Tin and two Rubber pins, and a deck of two Hammer, two Hook and two Spare Balls. Tin is worth half a Wood pin when it falls but pays for frames left standing, and Rubber scatters unpredictably, so the rack already asks what to replace first.
- **Specialist tags:** material, modification, repair, rack and economy, weight 2 (provisional until E24).

## E17-D17: the definitions the spec needs (3)
- **Grit** (`lane_condition.grit`, primitive `lane.front_friction`): more rolling friction on the front 30 ft, so the ball reaches the rack slower. The oil and hook are unchanged; a heavy ball or a harder throw carries through.
- **Warped Boards** (`lane_condition.warped_boards`, primitive `lane.board_drift`): between 20 and 40 ft the ball drifts steadily right. Aim left of the usual line, or hook back left.
- **Salvage Kit** (`kit.salvage`, `kit.loadout`).
- The three Salvage rules were written earlier: Frugal House and Dull Pins in E17-D03, Rust in E17-D05. The exact friction and drift are set and measured by the next claim, as E18-C13 did for Neon's lanes.
