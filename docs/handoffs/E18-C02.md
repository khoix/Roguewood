## Changes and decisions
- **Primitive.** A pin effect `{op: "transform", trigger: "fallen", amount: <whole percent 1–100>, into: "<pin id>", scope}` (catalogue v2):
  - It is deferred like a payout: it never changes the bank and is never retriggered.
  - It is reported only for pins that fell, and validated in FrameRules and the catalogue contract: a pin owner, a fall trigger, a whole percent, an existing other pin, no `per`.
  - The design's `pin.break_chance` is this transform.
- **Roll.**
  - `RunInventory.apply_frame` rolls one draw per transform on the attempt's effects stream (the E14 Lehmer generator). Slice and lab attempts seed it from the attempt seed; the campaign uses its saved `effects` stream state.
  - The broken pin becomes the `into` pin in the same rack slot. It keeps its modification; growth and paid price reset.
  - The stream state is saved as `run.effects_state`, an additive field that older saves lack and that loads unchanged. The frame_done checkpoint holds the rack after the breaks, so a reload never rerolls.
  - The frame's explanation adds "Glass Pin at N cracked: now a Wood Pin."; the score log names "20% chance to become Wood Pin".
- **Landed:**
  - pin.glass: +30 Value; a 20% chance to become Wood.
  - charm.glass_cutter: +4 Mult per Glass pin down.
  - Mechanics and automated validation are true. Artwork stays false: Codex's D01 art paths for Glass are in the entry, but in-play and activation slots need Codex's native check.
- **Determinism:** the same attempt seed breaks the same pins; another seed differs. Physics and presentation never draw on the stream.
