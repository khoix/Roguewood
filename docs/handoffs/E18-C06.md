## Changes and decisions
- **Instant tactics.** These three cards are played when tapped, before the throw, not with the launch, because each one changes the rack or the rules the throw meets.
  - Tapping one opens a chooser (TacticSheet): which pin, then which spot, or a confirmation. Cancel spends nothing.
  - On a choice the card is spent (`equipment.play`, so one tactical card per roll still holds), the action is applied and the attempt is saved at once.
  - The lab's existing armed tacticals (`consumable.lab_*`) still play with the launch. Arming one on a roll that already had a tactic is now refused with a note, instead of failing at commit.
- **Nudge** (`tactical.relocate_pin`): before roll 2 only. A standing pin moves onto an adjacent spot (one pin spacing) whose pin has fallen.
  - The pin keeps its identity (rack position), so its material, modification, physics profile, reports and scoring are unchanged.
  - The lane is rebuilt with the moved pin before the throw.
- **Reserve Call** (`tactical.reserve_swap`): at the start of a frame, before roll 1, and once per frame. A reserve pin swaps into a chosen rack position.
  - The swap is undone once, after the frame banks and before the frame's save. Growth and breaks from that frame travel with the pin that earned them.
  - A save taken mid-frame keeps the swap and its action, so a reload neither undoes nor repeats it.
- **Rule Waiver** (`tactical.waive_rule`): boss matches only, once per frame.
  - **Decision:** the item's authored text wins over the claim wording. The claim said "for one roll"; the item says "for the rest of the current frame", so a waiver lasts the rest of the frame.
  - All the rival's rules are suspended (a champion's two rules together): launch constraints (Dead Straight, House Rules), the scoring loadout (House Pins) and the bank (Clean Sweep).
  - Introductions, previews and results still name the rival's rules. The badge reads "— waived this frame".
- **Explanation:** each tactic adds one line ("◆") to the frame's explanation before the rule lines. The status line lists the tactics in force.
- **Saves:** `frame_actions` is an optional lab key. It is written only when a tactic was played and defaults to none, so older saves load unchanged.
- **Content:** the three items are landed (mechanics and automated validation true; D01 art paths kept, artwork and effects flags left to Codex). The Pro Shop's tactical slot, which was empty in production, now offers them from Division 1.
