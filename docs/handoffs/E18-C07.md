## Changes and decisions
- **The system (design §3 "Pro-shop licenses", §15).** Upgrades are bought from a new **License** offer, placed in the run's upgrades, and kept for the attempt: one of each, never sold, saved with the run (an additive `upgrades` key).
  - **Decision: one License per Division.** It is drawn at the Division's first shop visit, kept through rerolls and later visits (sold or not), and redrawn in the next Division, like Balatro's vouchers. Upgrades stay a considered choice rather than a reroll target.
  - Content without upgrades (the lab fixtures) has no License offer, so its shops are unchanged.
- **The six House upgrades** (E17-D06; amounts and prices provisional, E24):

  | Upgrade | Rarity, price | Earliest | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Punch Card | common $4 | D1 | The first reroll in each visit is free; then $2, $3, ... |
  | Pin Caddy | common $4 | D1 | A third reserve slot. |
  | Clearance Tag | uncommon $6 | D2 | Every offer, the License included, is $1 less (never under $1), at once. Sales stay half the price paid, so no resale profit. |
  | Savings Book | uncommon $6 | D2 | The interest cap rises from $3 to $5. |
  | Extra Shelf | uncommon $7 | D2 | A sixth offer of any category, from the next stock. |
  | Spotlight Sign | rare $8 | D3 | Rarity odds become 40/38/18/4 (from 55/30/12/3), from the next stock. |
- **Fixes along the way:**
  - The shop view asked `plan(offer.slot)` instead of `plan(index)`. These were equal only while every slot appeared once in order.
  - The authored test's "unknown host" mutation targeted the last batch; it now names D04.
- **Shop stream:** the License draw and C06's tactical slot (empty in production until C06) use the shop stream. Slice shops therefore offer different items from before; see the checkpoint for the journeys adjusted.
