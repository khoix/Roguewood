## The primitives
- **`physics.gutter_rebound`.**
  - The tracker decides: a ball that reaches the gutter edge before touching a pin, with a rebound left, is recorded as `rebounded` instead of a gutter ball. It stays pure (no physics access).
  - The lane turns the ball once: its speed down the lane is kept and its sideways speed is mirrored, as a bumper does (at least 0.15 m/s, so a ball creeping over the edge still returns). Measured: a throw that drifted out at 0.29 m/s comes back at 0.29 m/s and takes seven pins.
  - For 24 ticks (0.2 s) afterwards the ball may still be over the edge on its way back. Still out after that, or out again later, is a gutter ball: it rebounds once.
  - At Narrow Lane's ruled edge the rebound happens there.
  - Once a frame: the lab gives a roll no rebound if an earlier roll of the frame used it.
- **Op `lose_mult`:** Mult falls by the amount and never below 1. Bumper Guard's "−2 Mult, never below 1" is `{op: lose_mult, amount: 2, condition: {rebounded: true}}` on the modification. The score log words it "−2 Mult (never below 1)".
- **`oil_hold`** (a ball): the ball's friction zones are House Oil's on every lane. Long Oil and Two-Tone then roll exactly as House Oil (identical final states). Boss rules (Oil Slick's halving) still apply.
- **A modification's `spin_response`:** multiplies the hook the charge produces, the same arithmetic as Oil Slick. A scale of 0.5 rolls exactly as Oil Slick's halving; 1.3 hooks 0.281 m against 0.227 m. The aim guide bends with it.
- **Conditions:** `rebounded`, `felled_curved`, `first_hit` (docs/CONTRACTS.md).
