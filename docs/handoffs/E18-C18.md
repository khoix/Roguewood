## The primitives (docs/CONTRACTS.md, "E18-C18")
- **Condition `accurate`:** on a roll's own effects, that roll was graded sweet or perfect; elsewhere, any roll was.
- **Career metric `controlled_curves`:** a strike whose first roll was curved and graded sweet or perfect, at career or attempt scope. Older Careers read 0.
- **Upgrade `sell_bonus` (Trade-In):** a sale returns $1 more, never more than was paid. Kit and reward items, which cost nothing, take the $1 on their base sale value.
- **Upgrade `specialist_offer` (Specialist Shelf):** one more offer, in a new shop slot 7 named "Specialist". It can be any category, but only items carrying the League's specialist tags. It is never offered in a League without specialists (House).
