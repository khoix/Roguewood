## The objectives and their metrics (docs/CONTRACTS.md, unlock registry)
- **Both Halves** (`mastery.both_halves`, attempt scope, metric `two_tone_halves`, threshold 2): on a Two-Tone lane, strike once from each half in one attempt. A Two-Tone strike's half is its first roll's side at 34 ft (the knockdown log's `left_side`). The attempt's tally keeps the halves struck; an older tally without them reads 0.
- **Rebound Rescue** (`mastery.rebound_rescue`, Career scope, metric `rescues`, threshold 3): three spares or strikes with a roll that rebounded off the gutter edge.
