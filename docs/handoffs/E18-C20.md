## The rules
`RivalRules` keeps each rule's effect outside the frame's calculation where it can, so the explanation stays readable.
- **Frugal House** (`no_item_income`): `paid(rules, result)` takes every payout out of what the run is credited, and returns what the house kept. The resolved result is unchanged. In the log each payout line ends "· kept by the house (Frugal House)" (and is marked `struck` for the art to strike through), and a rule line names the total ("$2 kept by the house"). Match pay and interest are not item income and still come.
- **Dull Pins** (`pin_value_scale`): `frame_context()` adds `pin_value_scale` 0.5 to the frame, and FrameRules scales every `add_value` whose source is a pin, rounded down per effect.
  - That covers the pin's own effects, its modification's, and every copy or retrigger.
  - Ball Value, Charm Value and every Mult are untouched.
  - The event keeps `dulled_from`, and each log step reads "(Dull Pins: half of 10, rounded down)".
  - Examples: a Wood strike banks (40 + 10 × 5) × 4 = 360 instead of 560; a Tin pin's 5 becomes 2.
- **Rust** (`no_mods`): `frame_loadout()` drops the rack's pin modifications from the scored rack, and `mods_off()` tells the lab to play no ball modification, in the scoring or in the roll (Bumper Guard's rebound and a modification's hook scale do nothing). The pins, balls and Charms work as normal, and the run keeps every modification.
- The rules combine: the champion's Rust plus Dull Pins scores a bare, dulled rack. Each Salvage rule adds a line to the frame's log, and the status line names the rules in force.

## The lanes
Both push on the ball; neither moves the oil, so House Oil, its friction and the hook are exactly as before (physics digest `75d1a3c0…` unchanged). A ball with `oil_hold`, which meets every lane as House Oil, is not pushed.
- **Grit** (`lane.front_friction`): on the front 30 ft (9.14 m) a drag of 1.0 N per m/s acts against the ball's travel. It is a force, so a heavier ball slows less.
  - Measured on the pocket throw, the House ball meets the head pin 45 ticks (0.75 s) later, and a 1.6× mass ball 26 ticks later.
  - The pocket strike leaves the 8 pin.
  - The slowest throw still reaches the rack.
  - A slower ball spends longer in the backend, so a curve hooks more on Grit; that is the physics, and the spec's text does not promise otherwise.
- **Warped Boards** (`lane.board_drift`): between 20 and 40 ft (6.10 to 12.19 m) the ball is pushed toward the right gutter at 0.12 m/s², whatever its mass.
  - Measured on a straight line from the centre, it reaches the pins 0.122 m further right.
  - J03C20's strike is aimed 0.12 left of the usual line on this lane.
  - A ball already in a gutter is not pushed.
