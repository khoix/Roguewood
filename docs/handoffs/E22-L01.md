## Decisions
- **Content sets.**
  - `house` is the new production set and the game's default: content/leagues/house.json with the production catalogue and registry.
  - `slice` stays E13's two-Division vertical slice, which J06, J10, J11 and the item journeys play with `--content=slice`. It gets its own registry, content/fixtures/slice_unlocks.json:
    - production's, except that the slice is `league.house`;
    - the full League listed as `league.house_full`, so its rival gates are still awarded.
    - `qa.py content` fails if the rest drifts.
  - The campaign builds on `house` and the production registry. The menu offers it beside the House League.
- **Recurring rivals.**
  - `rivals_recur: true` in the spec lets LeagueRun accept a rival met again in a later Division (design §18). Without it, a repeat is still refused, as for the campaign.
  - A recurring rival's first clear counts once.
  - The Clubhouse names each source of a gate once ("Beat The Surveyor (House League)", not twice).
- **Boss lanes.**
  - Each boss plays one of its listed lanes, chosen by Division number: `lanes[(division - 1) % n]` (ContentSet.assign_lanes).
  - So a saved attempt always meets the same lane: Divisions 1, 3 and 5 on House Oil; 2, 4, 6, 7 and 8 on Dry Backend.
  - Openers and features keep the standard pattern.
  - A per-attempt lane stream can come with E24.
- **Gates and rewards.**
  - `gate.proprietor_cleared` unlocks House Special and `gate.old_lou_cleared` Wood Grain (from E18-C08/C09).
  - The champion's first clear awards a new `gate.house_champion_cleared`, a plaque only. A championship gate can only be awarded by the League title, which still awards `gate.house_champion` (Crown Pin, Split Specialist, Crown Jewel, Echo Charge and now the Tournament Kit).
- **Kits.**
  - The League lists its kits with their loadouts. The menu shows them, disabling one whose gate the Career has not earned ("· win the House League title").
  - The attempt starts with the chosen kit: Tournament is four ball cards and $10.
  - The Tournament Kit lands in the catalogue (primitive `kit.loadout`, batch label E18-C12).
- **Theme** (house set only; the slice's screens are unchanged):
  - LeagueTheme reads assets/leagues/house/manifest.json.
  - Each Division's environment scene sits at the lane origin in the lab viewport, replaced when the stage changes. The final boss match shows the Championship stage.
  - The match's lane condition picks the lane surface material, physics untouched.
  - The music loops at −18 dB on the Master bus.
  - A test that quits while the loop plays stops it and waits a few frames, or the audio thread still holds it at exit.
