## The notes and what changed
1. **"UI usage SFXs, like for button taps":**
   - Every button in the game sounds when pressed.
   - One hook on the SceneTree (`UiSounds`, a child of the shell) reaches each button as it is added, so new screens need no wiring.
   - There are three sounds:
     - **tap** for most buttons;
     - **select** for toggles and the Clubhouse tabs;
     - **back** for Close, Done, Back, Resume and Cancel.
   - A button can opt out, or pick its own sound, with the meta `ui_sound`.
   - The taps follow the sound-effects slider and Mute.
   - The three WAVs are provisional wood foley from tools/author_foley.py. The final set is Codex's (brief below).
2. **"The post-frame pin tally log… gets smaller again in later frames":**
   - **Cause:** the log was sized to fit its single longest line. A later frame's longer trigger line therefore shrank the whole log.
   - **Fix:** the size now fits the line at the 75th percentile of the frame's lines (`LOG_FIT_SHARE`). The few longer lines wrap.
3. **"Subtly decrease the music volume to 50% (not SFX) while in Pro Shop or Options":**
   - In a match, the League music eases to half (−6 dB) over 0.4 s while the Pro Shop or Options is open, and eases back when it closes.
   - The Clubhouse music does the same under the Clubhouse's Options.
   - Sound effects keep their level.
4. **"Add hamburger menu to Clubhouse":**
   - A three-bar button sits at the Clubhouse's top right, above the tabs.
   - It opens Options and Sound & effects, then Close. These are the same pages as in a match, on the same session settings, so a change made in the Clubhouse is what the next match uses.
   - The pages are rebuilt on each opening, so they also show changes made in a match.
5. **"Change 'Attempt' language to 'Run'":**
   - Every player-facing "attempt" now reads "run". This covers Continue Run, Restart run, the Runs tile, "from your next run", "in one run", "kept for the run" and the record lines.
   - Save keys, identifiers and node names are unchanged (`attempt`, `career_attempt`, `ContinueAttempt`).
6. **"Instead of 'Try Again', make it 'New Run'":** the League Board's button reads "New Run" once a League has been played, and the end screen's after a win.
7. **"The menu for applying charms and such to pins should be visual":**
   - Placing an item shows a map of the rack: the ten pins in the bowling triangle, back row first, each with its art and its modification's badge. The other places (reserves, Charm slots, the deck) appear as tiles below.
   - Pins that are not a choice are shown dimmed and cannot be pressed.
   - A tap selects a place. It then shows what is there now and what will change, for example "Rack 1 · now: Wood / Rack 1 — Wood moves to Reserve 1". Confirm (Place, Attach, Replace or Remove) applies it.
   - Keyboard and gamepad focus shows the same detail, and accept applies it, as before.
   - The Remove-a-card sheet uses the same tiles.
8. **"Sell menu should be visual":**
   - Sell shows the owned items as tiles: art, modification badge, name and price.
   - A tap opens the item large, with its place, modification and sale price, and "Sell +$N" or Back.
   - Accept on a focused tile still sells at once, and the focused tile's line ("Wood · Reserve 1 · sells for $1") shows under the tiles.
   - The Charm order and its moves sit under the tiles.
   - In modification_art.gd, the Sell rows' 28 px host icon is no longer forced onto these tiles (meta `art_tile`). The badge is still added, now sized to the tile.

## Codex brief: the final UI sound set (proposed AV-UI1)
- Replace assets/audio/ui_tap.wav, ui_select.wav and ui_back.wav, keeping their names, so no code changes.
- Original and procedural, in the House's wood-and-brass hall and in the existing foley's character.
- Each is 30–80 ms, mono and peak-normalised like the foley. They should sit under the foley, and the game plays them at 0.8 of the effects level.
- The roles:
  - **tap:** a light, dry wooden press;
  - **select:** a slightly brighter tick for toggles and tabs;
  - **back:** a softer, lower close.
- **Optional:** per-League variants (Neon's synthetic click) as `ui_tap.neon.wav` and so on, if Codex wants them. The hook would pick them by League (a small code change, Claude's).
