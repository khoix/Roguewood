## What it is
A guided practice match on the real lane, not slides. Each step works the same way:
- it dims the screen except what it teaches;
- an animated pixel hand shows the gesture;
- one short line of text explains;
- then it waits for the player to do it in the real game, or for Next on a step that only explains.

**The steps:**
1. **Welcome.**
2. **Win the match:** the top bar, with this match's target and frames.
3. **Your cards:** tap one to throw it; hold one to read it.
4. **Aim:** tap the lane.
5. **Throw:** hold, swirl in circles to charge a curve, flick up.
6. **Roll again:** only after a roll that leaves pins.
7. **Scoring:** Value × Mult is banked.
8. **Next frame:** tap Next Rack.
9. **Your turn:** the second frame, without dimming.
10. **Roll again:** only after a roll that leaves pins.
11. **Collect & Shop.**
12. **The Pro Shop.**
13. **Buy the Brass Cap**, a pin modification.
14. **Buy:** in the offer sheet.
15. **Place it:** on the rack map; the hand points at a pin, then at Confirm.
16. **Sell:** open Sell.
17. **Your items:** the Sell list; the hand points at Done.
18. **The boss:** The Surveyor's badge and rule.
19. **You're ready.**

Steps 8 and 9 are skipped when the first frame already wins the match. Only the main steps are numbered ("Step k of 14"), so the count never jumps.

**Behaviour:**
- **Starts:**
  - on a brand-new player's first Enter League (the real game only: not a shell embedded by a journey, and not the browser QA run);
  - and any time from the Clubhouse menu's first entry, How to Play.
- **Skip:** offered at every step. From the first-run start, Skip and "Start your run" go on to the League the player chose; from How to Play they return to the Clubhouse. "Keep practising" leaves the practice lane playing, unguided.
- **Losing the practice opener:** "Not this time" offers Try again (a fresh lane, starting at Aim) or Skip. It appears once the frame's review has finished, because a press on a review that is still counting up skips it.
- **Reduced motion:** every step stays; the hand holds one pose, and the gesture's path is drawn in full.
- **Explain-only steps:** the lane ignores touches. The lab also treats the coach's card as UI on every step, so a touch on it is never a throw.
- **Over full-screen sheets:** the rack map and the Sell list show no card. The sheet's own prompt says what to do, and the hand shows where.

## The fixed practice lane
- **Content set `tutorial`:** the production catalogue, one short House Division (Opener 40, Feature 150, Boss The Surveyor 300; two frames a match) and a Practice Kit (the House pins and deck, $10). It wears the House theme (`theme`), and its rival grants nothing.
- **Fixed every time:**
  - the seed (`TUTORIAL_SEED` 7), so the same cards are dealt;
  - the first Pro Shop visit sells `tutorial_offers` (Flat Bonus, Pin Counter, Gold Pin, Nudge, Brass Cap), priced by the shop's rules.
- **Nothing counts:**
  - the lab plays on a throwaway Career (`CareerProfile.fresh()`) with saving off (`save_store = null`): no runs, unlocks, records or saved run;
  - the real Career gains only `tutorial: {seen, completed}`. That is a new key; older saves load as before, and validation does not require it.
- **Art:** the hand and spotlight are drawn by code in the game's pixel style. Polished art can replace them later (an optional Codex item); nothing waits on it.
