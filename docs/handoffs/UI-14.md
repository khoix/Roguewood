## What it is
While the player moves the target, the top of the screen shows a zoomed-in view of exactly what the player sees down the lane, around the pin deck, at better quality. Owner (2026-11-18): "I simply want a zoomed in view of exactly what it would look like if the player were down the alley … I don't need a new ring, I want to see the existing one." The owner's zoomed screenshot set the framing.

**When it shows:**
- while a finger drags the target on the lane;
- while a finger is held on the lane (0.15 s), before any drag;
- while the assist panel's target slider is dragged.

A quick tap that just places the target does not open it. It fades in and out in 0.12 s and is gone once the finger lifts. It never shows outside aiming: not while the ball is held, rolling, or in a menu.

**Where:**
- **Portrait:** over the top HUD, at the top bar's position and full width, and taller than the bar (2.8 : 1), so it also covers the row below for that moment.
- **Landscape:** the top bar is a thin strip, so the view is a 2.8 : 1 box at the bar's left end, at most a quarter of the screen tall. It leaves the lane's real pins in view.

**The view:**
- **An exact crop of the player's view.** A second camera on the lab's own 3D world copies the lane camera's pose each frame and uses an off-axis frustum, so its picture is the lane's picture, cropped. It uses the same lane, pins, lighting, target marker and lane art, with nothing added to the world.
- **Better quality:** the lane renders at half resolution for its pixel look, while the magnifier renders at its own full resolution.
- **The existing ring:** a second copy of the lane's own aim overlay (ring, Neon zone marks and rails), scaled and shifted by the same crop. It is the same ring, zoomed with its stroke, and J02 checks it sits exactly where the zoomed lane shows the target.
- **The crop:** centred on the lane at the head pin. The lane there spans 44% of the magnifier's width, and the head pin's base sits 80% of the way down. That is the owner's reference: the ring's top arc and the target marker over the rack.
- **Zoom:** 6.0× in portrait and 5.0× in landscape (logged by J02, which requires at least 4×).
- **Not shown:** the 2D backdrop behind the lane (the night sky) is not in the crop. The lane's surroundings at the pin deck are 3D, so little of it would show.

**Cost:** while hidden, its view renders nothing (`UPDATE_DISABLED`); while shown, one small extra view of the lane.

## Engine note
The lab gives each roll a fresh 3D world, and the magnifier follows it. When a viewport's world is replaced, Godot 4.5 drops its reference to the old world before detaching the old scenario. If the magnifier held the last reference, the scenario was already freed and Godot logged `Parameter "scenario" is null`. The magnifier now holds the old world until the swap is done.
