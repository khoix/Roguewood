## What it is
While the player moves the target, the top of the screen shows a close, low view of the front of the rack, with the target ring drawn on the deck. The ring moves across the view as the target moves across the lane.

**When it shows:**
- while a finger drags the target on the lane;
- while a finger is held on the lane (0.15 s), before any drag;
- while the assist panel's target slider is dragged.

A quick tap that just places the target does not open it. It fades in and out in 0.12 s and is gone once the finger lifts. It never shows outside aiming: not while the ball is held, rolling, or in a menu.

**Where:**
- **Portrait:** over the top HUD, at the top bar's position and full width, and taller than the bar (2.8 : 1), so it also covers the row below for that moment.
- **Landscape:** the top bar is a thin strip, so the view is a 2.8 : 1 box at the bar's left end, at most a quarter of the screen tall. It leaves the lane's real pins in view.

**The view:**
- a second camera on the lab's own 3D world: the same lane, pins, lighting and lane art, with nothing added to the world;
- the camera is fixed behind the target line, over the lane's centre. It frames the lane's full width and the head pin from base to top, and fits itself to the panel's shape;
- about 8 to 10 times the main view's scale at the target line: J02 logs 9.6× in portrait and 8.0× in landscape, and requires at least 5×.

**The ring is drawn at its true size:** the band the ball's centre must cross at the target line to count as accurate (`ring_radius` × the lane half-width). The main view's reticle is a fixed on-screen size for readability, so it looks larger than the band really is; the magnifier shows the true band against the pins.

**Cost:** while hidden, its view renders nothing (`UPDATE_DISABLED`); while shown, one small extra view of the lane.

## Engine note
The lab gives each roll a fresh 3D world, and the magnifier follows it. When a viewport's world is replaced, Godot 4.5 drops its reference to the old world before detaching the old scenario. If the magnifier held the last reference, the scenario was already freed and Godot logged `Parameter "scenario" is null`. The magnifier now holds the old world until the swap is done.

## Known limits
- **Lanes that change the target's band:** the ring is drawn as `ring_radius` × the lane half-width. A rule that scales the accuracy zones (Neon's Strobe `zone_scale`) changes the grade bands inside the ring, not the ring itself. The magnifier shows the ring only, as the main view's reticle does, without the Neon zone marks.
- **Device feel:** the 0.15 s hold and the size are first choices; the owner's phone check is the evidence.
