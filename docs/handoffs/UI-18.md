# Agent task handoff — UI-18, the inspected card tilts as one unit (owner, 2026-11-19)

## Assignment
- **Owner:** Claude, at the owner's request: "When looking at cards, the 'drag to tilt' feature only shifts the image framed by the card, it's not tilting the card itself like it's one unit."
- **State:** DONE.
- **Branch:** `claude/roguewood-build`.
- **Paths:** src/presentation/card_inspector.gd; src/presentation/card_tilt.gdshader (new); tests/motion_journey.gd (J08).

## What changed
- **Before:** a drag rotated only the illustration inside the card a little.
- **Now:** the whole card renders into its own view (`TiltView`), and a perspective shader turns it toward the finger. The frame, text, art and rarity material all move together, up to 0.3 rad (about 17°) at the card's edge. The side under the finger leans away.
- **Room for the turn:** the view is 8% larger than the card on each side, so the nearer corners never clip. The card's place in the layout (`CardSlot`) is unchanged, so the panel is the same size.
- **Sharpness:** the card renders at the screen's own pixel density, so it is as sharp as before when flat.
- **Unchanged behaviour:**
  - the rarity material's phase still follows the tilt (UI-A2);
  - under Reduced Motion the card stays flat;
  - off the card it settles flat.
- **Hint:** it now reads "Drag the card to tilt it".

## For tools and tests
- `CardInspector.tilt_area()` is the card's place on screen, for pointer tests (the art now lives inside the card's own view).
- **J08 now checks:**
  - the picture does not move separately;
  - the card's view carries the tilt;
  - the card turns toward the pointer, but not under Reduced Motion;
  - the view has room around the card.
