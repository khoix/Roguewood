## What the player sees
**The alley screen.** The aim magnifier's window (UI-14) doubles as the alley's overhead screen:
- **Portrait:** over the top HUD, full width, 2.8 : 1.
- **Landscape:** a 2.8 : 1 box at the top bar's left end.

When a celebration plays, that window shows a small CRT screen running a short, cheerfully cheesy animation in the League's own style.

| Event | When it plays | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **Strike** | All ten pins fall on the frame's first roll, as soon as the roll settles | about 1.8 s |
| **Spare** | The second roll clears the rest | about 1.6 s |
| **Boss defeated** | The boss match is won, when its last frame's tally finishes | about 3 s |

**Behaviour:**
- **Never on an open frame,** and never in practice, the tutorial, or a restored frame that is only being shown again.
- **Never delays the game or changes a score.** Strikes and spares play over the roll's result and the start of the tally, which go on as normal. For the boss, the Division Result button appears when the celebration ends or is skipped. A tap on the screen skips any celebration.
- **The boss win names the rival** and uses the rival's own portrait (assets/rivals/). Under it, the rival's "defeated" reaction plays as it does now.
- **The CRT look** applies to all three, so every celebration feels like the alley's overhead TV: subtle scanlines, a slight curve at the edges, a soft glow and a vignette. The owner required it for the boss win; the strike and spare share it for consistency.
- **Sound:** a short sting per event, from the League's set, at the Sound Effects level. Silent under Mute. The music does not stop.
- **Accessibility:**
  - **Reduced Motion:** a still card (the event's title art in the League's style) for the same length, with no movement.
  - **Reduced Flash:** no flicker, no rapid brightness change, and nothing faster than 3 flashes a second in any mode.
  - The scanlines stay subtle in every mode.

## Each League's own celebrations
- **Where they live:** each League theme's manifest (assets/leagues/<league>/manifest.json) gains a `celebrations` entry naming one scene per event:
  `{"celebrations": {"strike": "res://…/strike.tscn", "spare": "res://…/spare.tscn", "boss_defeated": "res://…/boss_defeated.tscn"}}`
- **This wave:** House and Neon (UI-A9).
- **Later Leagues:** Salvage, Royal, Spectral and Foundry get theirs with their League themes (E22-L03-ART onward), to the standard UI-A9 writes down.
- **Fallback:** a League without a scene for an event plays Claude's placeholder (plain pixel text in the League's UI colours). It is never silent and never fails.

## The scene contract (shared with Codex)
**The canvas:** each celebration scene is a `Control` sized to a fixed pixel canvas of **252 × 90** (2.8 : 1).
- The alley screen renders it in its own SubViewport at that size, then scales it with nearest-neighbour filtering to the window, under the CRT shader.
- Art is authored for that canvas; the chunky low resolution is part of the look.

**The interface** the scene's script provides:

| Member | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `func play(context: Dictionary) -> void` | Starts the animation from the beginning. |
| `signal finished` | Emitted once at the natural end. |
| `func duration() -> float` | Seconds; must be within the ranges in the table above. |
| `func skip() -> void` | Jumps to the end, then emits `finished`. |

**`context`** carries:
- `event`, `league_id`, `division`, `championship` (bool);
- `rival_name`, `rival_id`, `rival_color` (boss only);
- `reduced_motion`, `reduced_flash`.

The scene draws the rival's portrait itself, from assets/rivals/manifest.json by `rival_id`.

**Rules for scenes:**
- No input handling (the alley screen owns taps).
- No audio of their own; the sting is played by the alley screen from the manifest's `celebration_sounds`.
- No access to game state beyond `context`.

## Where Claude's part goes
- **AlleyScreen** (alley_screen.gd): a sibling of AimMagnifier with the same placement. It holds:
  - a SubViewport at 252 × 90 (`UPDATE_DISABLED` while hidden);
  - the CRT shader on its container;
  - the skip tap;
  - the sting;
  - a queue, so a celebration never overlaps another, and the magnifier never shows while one plays.
- **Triggers** in throw_lab.gd: strike and spare from the roll result (as the frame log already knows them); boss defeated in `_on_scored` when the boss match is won.
- **League theme:** `celebration(event) -> PackedScene` reads the manifest, falling back to the placeholder.
- **Saves:** unchanged. Celebrations are presentation only and are not saved; a frame restored after a restart does not replay one.
