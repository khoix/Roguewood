## What the player sees
When a run ends (won or lost) having earned something, the ceremony plays over the result sheet. Continue then reveals the result sheet as before.

**"Trophy Night":**
- a velvet-dark stage with a slow gold sunburst;
- a spotlight;
- chunky gold lettering;
- confetti in the club's colours, with Neon's pink and cyan in the Neon League.

**The sequence:**
1. **Opening:** "AWARDS" drops in with a whoosh, and three bottle rockets go up. A run that only moved goals opens on "PROGRESS", more quietly: no fireworks, a little confetti.
2. **Each new trophy, one at a time, with its own Clubhouse art:**
   - a League title ("League Champion!", or "Champion Again!" for a repeat);
   - a rival's plaque;
   - a Clubhouse decoration.

   Each has a drumroll under a gathering sunburst, a flash and a reveal chime as it pops up, and a shine across it. Confetti cannons fire and fireworks burst, each with its launch and burst sounds. A League title gets the full show; plaques and decorations a quicker one. Each trophy then moves to a shelf.
3. **Goals:**
   - **Progress:** every goal the run moved, with its ribbon. Its bar fills from where it stood before the run to where it stands now, one rising tick per step (at most 16).
   - **A goal finished:** the bar turns gold, a "COMPLETE!" stamp slams down, and confetti bursts from the row with a bottle rocket.
   - **Wording:** Career goals show "4 → 7 / 10". Single-run goals say "This run: 1 / 3 · starts over next run", honestly.
4. **Finale:** a confetti rain and a volley of fireworks, then Continue.

**Controls:**
- **Tap:** hurries the current moment along.
- **Skip** (bottom centre, where Continue appears): goes straight to the summary, with everything in its final place.
- **Escape:** skips, then continues.

**When it does not play:** nothing earned, the tutorial, practice (which banks no trophies or goals), and a result restored after a restart (shown as before).

**Accessibility:**
- **Reduced Motion:** no travelling effects at all (no particles, rockets or sunburst spin). Elements appear in place, with the chimes and stamp sounds only.
- **Reduced Flash:** no flash, no shine sweep and no fireworks; confetti stands in for them.

## What counts (RunAwards, from the Career)
Each award already records the run it came from, so the ceremony is right even for a run resumed after a restart:
- **League titles:** `championships[league].last_attempt`.
- **Plaques:** `first_clears[rival].career_attempt`.
- **Decorations:** a milestone count that crossed its threshold this run.
- **Completed goals:** `mastery[goal].career_attempt`. A completed goal is presented with its ribbon.
- **Goal progress:** Career totals minus this run's tally (Career goals), or this run's tally (single-run goals).

Up to six goals are shown, completed ones first, then tracked ones.

## Music: the place for the owner's track
- **The slot:** `Soundtrack.TRACKS["awards"]` → `res://assets/audio/music/awards.ogg`.
- **While the ceremony shows:** the track fades in and loops at the Music level, and the League's music steps aside (to silence). Without the track, the League's music just eases to 40%.
- **Adding it:** drop the Suno MP3 as `awards.mp3`. Claude runs `python3 tools/author_soundtrack.py awards.mp3` (a seamless loop) and imports it with looping on, as for MUSIC-1.
