## What the player sees
**How to Play** in the Clubhouse menu now opens a page of the menu with two choices:
- **Play Tutorial:** the practice lane (UI-13), as before ("A guided practice lane").
- **Talk the Talk:** the guide ("Bowling words, explained with pictures").
- **Back:** returns to the menu. Escape does the same.

A new player's first Enter League still offers the tutorial directly, as before.

**Talk the Talk** has five tabs (labelled Run, Frames, Lane, Scoring and Cards so they fit a phone; Next names them in full), each a list of words. Every word has a picture, its name and a line or two on what it means in Roguewood:

| Tab | Words |
|---|---|
| The Run | League, Run, Division, Match, Opener, Feature, Boss, Rival, Championship |
| Frames | Frame, Strike, Spare, Open frame, Leave, Split, Double, Turkey, Gutter ball |
| Lane & Ball | Rack, Head pin, Pocket, Corner pins, Back row, Hook, Curved or straight, Charge, Oil, Backend, Boards, Sweet & Perfect |
| Scoring | Value, Mult, Bank, Retrigger, Payout |
| Your Cards | Pro Shop, Ball cards, Pins, Charms, Tactical, Mods, Upgrades, Kit, Binder, Goals, Trophies |

**Moving around:**
- Each tab ends with **Next** to the following tab, so the guide can be walked through in order.
- **Back** (always visible at the bottom) or Escape returns to How to Play.
- The Clubhouse music eases to half while the guide is open, as under Throw Settings.
- **Layout:** in portrait, the full width; in landscape, a centred column.

## The pictures (drawn, in the game's own visual language)
- **The rack:** as the frame review draws it. The head pin is nearest; a pin taken by roll 1 is orange, one taken by roll 2 is blue, and a standing pin is ivory with its red neck stripe. The frame's mark (X, 8 /, 8 1) sits in a score box.
- **Score-sheet frames:** used for Frame, Double and Turkey.
- **A Division's three steps and a League's eight:** with the boss's crown and the trophy.
- **The lane from above:** its 39 boards, the oil (to 40 ft of 60, as House Oil), the backend, gutters, and a ball's line (straight, hooking or into the gutter).
- **The held ball:** with its swirl and the charge meter.
- **The target ring:** its Clean, Sweet and Perfect bands, in the ring's own colours and at its real proportions.
- **Value × Mult = Bank:** the counters, using the same 140 × 4 = 560 strike example as the text.
- **Cards:** the card faces' own icons, with price tags, a fan of ball cards, and Charms in order.
- **Art:** The Surveyor and their plaque, and the House and Neon trophies, from the Clubhouse and rival art.

**Pictures follow the player's hand:** Pocket, Hook, Charge and the lanes are mirrored for a left-hander (Throw Settings). The pocket's text names both sides.

## Accuracy
Each meaning was checked against the game's rules and content:
- **Scoring:** frame bases 40/4, 20/2 and 0/1; a Wood pin is 10 Value; the accuracy bonus is +1 or +2 Mult, best roll of the frame, once.
- **The run:** openers always on House Oil (every League's openers have no lane of their own); the strike streak is the run's, so it carries from match to match; Charms score left to right and can be reordered in the Pro Shop.
- **The lane:** 39 boards; Narrow Lane's two boards.
- **The bowling meanings:** pocket (1-3 for a right-hander, 1-2 for a left-hander), split, double, turkey and leave match common bowling usage.
  - **Sources:** bowl.com (USBC), Bowling.com, the PBA and Wikipedia glossaries.
  - **Not quoted directly:** this session's network blocked Wikipedia and Kegel. The split wording follows the USBC rule (head pin down, a gap between the pins left) as commonly cited, not the rulebook itself.
