## What changed
- **Scrolling:** a finger that lands anywhere on the list scrolls it, a word's row or the Next button included. The rows had stopped drags, the Binder's old problem (UI-8). The fix uses the Clubhouse's `drag_through` and passing rows. As on the Clubhouse's pages, the list scrolls with no bar.
- **Tabs:** Run, Frames, Lane, Scoring and Cards are now the Clubhouse's own tabs. They sit as segments in a dark track, with the chosen one lit and underlined in brass, replacing the five framed buttons.
- **A tapped word opens large:** an overlay shows the word at 36 px, its picture at full width (up to 520 px, about three times the list's), and its meaning at 24 px.
  - **Closing it:** Close, a tap outside it, or Escape. Escape closes the word first and then the guide.
  - **Keyboard:** Enter on a focused row opens it.
  - **Scrolling:** a press that ends in a scroll opens nothing.
