## Verified
It did. Buying a pin for a place whose pin had a modification built a fresh pin with none. The modification left with the old pin, to a reserve or into the sale.

## Fix
A pin placed where a modified pin stands now takes over its modification, and what was paid for it.
- **Old pin to a reserve:** it moves without the modification.
- **Old pin replaced and sold:** it sells for the pin alone; the modification is not sold.
- **Rack and reserve places alike.**
- **An excluded pairing:** if the new pin and the modification exclude each other (Gilt Leaf and the Gold pin), the modification stays with the old pin, as before.
- **The placement sheet says so:**
  - the place's line reads, for example, "Rack 2 — Wood moves to Reserve 2 (Lab Oak stays on the new pin)";
  - its detail adds "After: Lab Gold + Lab Oak";
  - its preview keeps the modification's badge on the new pin.

**Rules:** `RunInventory.carried_mod(item_id, target)`; `placements()` adds `keeps_mod` and prices `sale` without the carried modification; `sale_value(ref, with_mod := true)`. A Glass pin that breaks already kept its modification.
