## 1. Only for real progress
**Why the page appeared for nothing:** a goal counted within one run, such as Mixed Rack ("This run: 1 / 3 · starts over next run"), was shown whenever the run moved it at all. Its count starts over the next run, so that was no progress. A run whose only "progress" was such a count opened the award page.

**Now:** a within-run goal shows only when the run completed it.

**The page opens for:**
- a new trophy, plaque or Clubhouse decoration;
- a Career goal moved forward;
- any goal completed.

**A League title won again** is no new award (owner, 2026-11-20: "Only for new awards"). It no longer opens the page or appears on it, so "Champion Again!" is gone.

Otherwise the result sheet keeps New Run or Retry and Clubhouse, as before.

## 2. The fade, halved
Leaving a run for the Clubhouse now fades out over 1.5 s, then 0.15 s in the dark, about 1.65 s in all (was 3.3 s). There is still no fade into the Clubhouse. The values are `EXIT_OUT` and `EXIT_HOLD` in bootstrap.gd.

## 3. The show, the whole time
The ceremony now runs a continuous show from its first moment until it is gone, including on the summary while it waits for Continue, and under the fade after it.
- **Fireworks all the time:** a shell every 0.24–0.5 s, of five kinds:
  - a peony;
  - a clean double ring;
  - a drooping gold willow;
  - a crossette that splits again;
  - a crackling glitter.
- **Salvos:** every 2.6–4.2 s, three shells at once and a fan of five bottle rockets.
- **Confetti from the top the whole time:** chips (not 2 px specks) across the full width, tumbling as they fall.
- **Cannons:** both bottom corners fire every 2.2–3.4 s, more than before.
- **Streamers:** fourteen paper streamers drift and sway down the screen, over and over.
- **The finale:** a grand salvo and a confetti rain on top of the show. This replaces the single confetti burst at the bottom for a goals-only page.
- **Sound:** the show's launches, bursts and rockets play on four voices of their own, a little under the sequence's sounds, never more than one every 0.12 s of the ceremony's own time, and at varied pitch. They never cut a trophy's reveal, and they carry on after Skip.
- **Phones:** at most eight shells glow at once. The show's own shells flash the screen only faintly; the full flash stays for a trophy's reveal.
- **Unchanged:**
  - **Reduced Motion:** no travelling effects, the show included.
  - **Reduced Flash:** confetti stands in for every firework.
