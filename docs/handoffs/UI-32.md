## What changes for the player
### The Inventory
- **The button:** the shop's "Sell · N · order" button is now **Inventory**, and so is its sheet's title.
- **Charms first:** a top section, "Charms · scored left to right", holds the Charms in one row in scoring order. Each tile shows its slot number, art, name and sale price; five fit a phone's width.
- **Reorder by drag and drop:**
  - Drag a Charm onto another slot and it moves there; the others close up.
  - The dragged tile dims, and the slot under the finger is ringed in brass.
  - Releasing a drag never opens the item.
  - The Move buttons are gone.
  - With keyboard or gamepad focus, Shift+Left or Shift+Right moves the focused Charm one slot.
- **Your Items:** the other items (deck cards, reserve pins) sit below, as before.
- **Tap any item,** a Charm included, to review it and sell it, as before. The sheet stays open across moves and sales.
- **The tutorial's Sell step** now says "Open Inventory."

### The payout and rack slip
"Payout & Rack" (always shown in landscape) now opens a slip in the League's own kit frame, cream paper with dark inks, instead of two paragraphs of text:
- **MATCH N PAYOUT,** with the total the match earned in green, and its rows: Match payout, Interest (on $N held), Item income (during the match), and any notes such as Savings Book's cap.
- **YOUR RACK:**
  - the ten pins as the bowler sees them (back row first), each with its own art and its modification's badge;
  - the rack in words ("All 10 Wood");
  - the reserve slots, with their pins' art;
  - Charms and Deck counts as chips;
  - any upgrades kept for the run.
- **Opening it scrolls the slip into view.**
- **The old text stays,** hidden, in `summary` and `rack_label` (tools, tests and Codex's modified-rack art read it).

## Rules
`RunInventory.move_charm(from, to)` moves a Charm between any two slots (1-based; the others close up). It refuses slots outside the row and changes nothing. `move_charm_left` stays.
