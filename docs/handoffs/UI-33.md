## Why it looked static
The ball did turn: the held ball with its flywheel spin, and the rolling ball with its physics. Nothing on it showed that.
- **The finger holes faced the pins,** away from the camera, which sits behind the bowler.
- **The ball's own art,** each ball's identity material, looks alike from every side at phone size. A turning ball read as a still one.

## Fix
- **The grip faces the bowler.** The three finger holes are now on the camera side, a little larger (radius 21 mm), so:
  - the held pinwheel spin turns the grip in plain view, in depth and flat view;
  - the first moments of the roll carry it forward over the top.
- **Roll spots:**
  - four small ivory spots round the circle the forward roll turns, so the roll streams over the top of the ball the whole way down the lane;
  - one on each side, so a hook's turn reads too.
- **Visual only:**
  - **Physics unchanged:** the collision sphere is unchanged and the physics digest still passes.
  - **Item art still applies:** the ball's first mesh is still the one each item's identity material dresses.
  - **Reduced Motion:** the held ball stays still, as before.

## Tests
- **J02:**
  - the rolling ball carries its grip and roll spots, and turns more than 90° in 40 frames;
  - in flat spin, all three holes are nearer the camera than the ball's centre (they face the bowler), and the grip turns on screen with the held swirl (more than 20°).
- **PASS:**
  - `physics` (digest unchanged); J02 headless and rendered;
  - J08, JAVT01, JAVH3AC, JAVH3BC, JUIA1, JUIA2, J22L01ART, J22L02ART, J22L02ENV, JAVN1–3, JAVC04A, JAVC04B, J19A16 and J12V07, headless and rendered.

## For Codex

## Follow-up, UI-33b (owner's play notes, 2026-11-20, after PR #52)
"Some of the ball surfaces still are not turning realistically with the ball. For example, the spare ball. The stripe just stays up and down the entire time, even with a hook. Please check all ball types and make sure they are actually spinning appropriately."

### What the check found (every ball in the catalogue)
- **Spare Ball:** its stripe was painted in world orientation ("deliberately independent of body spin"), so it never turned.
- **Echo Ball:** its rings were centred on the axis a held spin turns about, so spinning it changed nothing on screen.
- **Laser Ball:** its line was about a pixel wide on a phone, so its turn barely showed.
- **The rest** (House, Hook, Hammer, Workhorse, Oilskin, Glow, and Comet on the default ball) were already painted on the ball.

### Fix
- **Spare Ball** (`assets/items/d09/equipment.gdshader`): the stripe is painted on the ball. Its band leans 35° off the forward-roll circle, so a straight roll sweeps it over as well as a hook or a held spin turning it.
- **Echo Ball** (`assets/items/balls/identity.gdshader`): the rings' axis leans off the spin axis, so they swing round as the ball spins.
- **Laser Ball** (`assets/items/neon/identity.gdshader`): the line and its tick are wider (0.0045 → 0.011, 0.0025 → 0.007).

### Test
- **J02, rendered:** every ball in the catalogue, with its grip and roll spots hidden, is turned 50° about the roll axis and 50° about the spin axis. Its own surface must change in at least 5% of the ball's pixels each time.
  - **Result** (share changed, rolled/spun): House 0.51/0.58, Hook 0.36/0.32, Hammer 0.45/0.57, Echo 0.23/0.25, Spare Ball 0.50/0.48, Workhorse 0.33/0.19, Oilskin 0.58/0.58, Laser 0.15/0.29, Glow 0.27/0.29, Comet 0.51/0.58.
  - **Before the fix:** Echo spun 0.01, and Laser 0.05/0.05.
  - **Contact sheet:** `ball_turns`, not committed.
- **PASS:** `unit`; J02 both modes; J08, JAVH3AC, JAVN1, JAVN1C, JAVN2, JAVN2C, J12V01, J12V07, J19A08, JAVC04B, JUIA1, JAVT01 and J22L01ART in both modes.
- **For Codex:** these three shaders are Codex's art; the coordinator changed them at the owner's request. A new ball's look must be painted in the ball's own coordinates, and must not be symmetric about the roll or spin axis. J02's check holds every ball to that.
