## What changes for the player
- **The item sheet:**
  - A tapped item opens with its full rules under its card, as its offer showed them: its effect, any tradeoff, and its modification's effect.
  - The grey line above them says what and where it is ("Hook Ball + Spin Wax · Deck card").
  - The price is only on the Sell button ("Sell +$2"); "sells for $" is gone from the item's line and from the Inventory's focus line.
- **Moving a Charm:**
  - **Lift:** press and hold a Charm for 0.3 s and it lifts into a card, a little larger and ringed in brass, that follows your finger. The empty slot it came from dims.
  - **Drop:** the slot under the card is ringed; releasing there moves the Charm, and the others close up. Releasing away from the row puts it back.
  - **Not a hold:** a press that moves more than 14 px before the hold is a swipe and lifts nothing. A release after a lift never opens the item.
  - **The hint** now says "Press and hold a Charm, then drag it to a new slot."
  - **Keyboard and gamepad:** Shift+Left/Right still moves a focused Charm.
  - **What it replaces:** UI-32's drag used Godot's built-in drag and drop. On a phone it showed nothing moving under the finger. The new lift is the game's own.
