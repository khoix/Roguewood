# Agent task handoff — UI-35, one scrim behind every overlay (owner, 2026-11-20)

## Assignment
- **Owner:** Claude, from the owner's play notes: "Bringing up the preview cards overlay in the inventory does not darken the background like others. Make sure that's consistent in the game when an overlay comes up."
- **State:** DONE.
- **Branch:** `claude/roguewood-build`.
- **Paths:**
  - `src/presentation/house_ui_theme.gd` (`SCRIM`) and `src/presentation/shop_panel.gd`;
  - the overlays: `card_inspector.gd`, `clubhouse.gd`, `clubhouse_options.gd`, `glossary_page.gd` and `bootstrap.gd`;
  - tests: `tests/shop_journey.gd` (J05) and `tests/clubhouse_journey.gd` (J11).

## Why
- **The shop's single dim stayed under every sheet.** An item opened from the Inventory sat over a bright Inventory.
- **The overlays' dims differed:**

| Overlay | Opacity before |
|---|---|
| Shop sheets | 0.6 |
| Menus, Options, Talk the Talk | 0.72 |
| Talk the Talk's word overlay | 0.82 |
| Binder preview | 0.86 |
| Card inspector | 0.96 |

## Fix
- **One scrim:** `UiTheme.SCRIM` (the menus' 0.72, dark blue-black) is now behind every overlay: the shop's sheets, the match and Clubhouse menus, Options, Talk the Talk and its word overlay, the Binder preview and the card inspector.
- **Stacked sheets darken what is under them:**
  - The shop's dim sits just under the topmost open sheet, and the newest sheet is drawn on top. An item over the Inventory darkens the Inventory.
  - Closing the item puts the dim back under the Inventory.
  - A tap on the dim closes only the top sheet: the item first, then an offer, then the Inventory.
- **One tap closes one sheet.** A tap reaches the dim twice, as touch and as Godot's emulated mouse, so one tap used to close both the item and the Inventory. The dim now takes one tap per frame.

## Tests
- **J05:**
  - with an item open over the Inventory, the dim is the game's scrim, between them;
  - after the sale, the dim is back under the Inventory;
  - a tap outside closes the item, not the Inventory.
- **J11:** the Binder preview, Talk the Talk and its word overlay use the scrim.
- **PASS:** `unit`, J01, J02, J05 (both modes), J06, J11 (both modes), J11ART and JTUT.
