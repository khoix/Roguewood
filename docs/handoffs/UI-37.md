## Why
UI-16 took the provisional House Campaign off the League Board; it is offered only beside the test slice (`--content=slice`, for J09). But the League records and the trophy shelf listed every League in the Career's graph (`content/career/unlocks.json`), so the game showed "House Campaign · not played yet" and an unwinnable trophy slot.

## What changes
- `ClubhouseModel.leagues(graph, sets)` picks the Leagues whose content set the board offers; `board()` and `trophies()` take those sets. With no sets they list every League, as before.
- The Clubhouse gets `league_sets` from the bootstrap (the board's choices): House and Neon in the game, the slice and the Campaign beside the test slice.
- Saves are unchanged: a Career that already holds Campaign records keeps them; they are only not shown.
- **Not changed:** the Campaign's own League, gate and title in the graph, and `RunAwards` (a Campaign title won in J09 still awards).
