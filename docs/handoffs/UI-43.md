## What changes for the player
- **The aim hint:**
  - one line in a pill just above the cards ("Hold the ball, swirl to curve, flick up · tap the lane to aim"), at 17 px in portrait;
  - it stays until the ball is touched, and comes back at the next aim;
  - a cancelled hold does not bring it back. The Tactic chooser's own instruction shows while it is open.
- **Notices are toasts:**
  - **What:** the rule in force, the lane, a tactic in play, and alerts (a refused card, a cancelled throw). Each is a brief toast above the hint, one at a time, about 2.2 s each, the rest queued.
  - **When:** at the start of every aim, and when a new alert comes; an alert goes to the front.
  - **Reduced motion:** a toast disappears without fading.
- **While the ball is held, rolls and a frame scores:** no text over the lane.
- **Space:** the stack never rises above the ball's lower edge.
  - One toast plus the hint fit on a phone; a stack of several would reach the ball, which is why toasts take turns.
  - Where the hand sits higher and both do not fit, the toast takes the hint's place while it shows.
  - With the assist panel open, the stack keeps to the free side beside it.
  - The stack breaks its own lines, word by word, so its size is exact as soon as a toast appears.
- **Ball cards a rule forbids** (House Rules) are dimmed in the hand. A tap still says why, as a toast.
- **Unchanged:** the status text, which tests and logs read; it keeps every line.
