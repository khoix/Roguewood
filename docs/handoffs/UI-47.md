## What changes for the player
- **Opening:** a tap on a Charm, or the kit, in the match's Charm rail opens a parchment bubble beside the rail, toward the lane. It points at the tapped piece and sits level with it, kept on screen. It shows:
  - the item's name;
  - its rarity and kind ("Uncommon Charm");
  - its rules: the card's own effect text, and any tradeoff.
- **Changing or closing:**
  - a tap on another Charm switches to it;
  - the same Charm again closes it;
  - a tap anywhere else only closes it (that tap does not move the target or throw);
  - starting a throw, the roll, or the shop closes it;
  - it also closes by itself after 6 s.
- **Where it opens:** while aiming, choosing a Tactic, or reviewing a frame. The rail never starts a throw or moves the target.
